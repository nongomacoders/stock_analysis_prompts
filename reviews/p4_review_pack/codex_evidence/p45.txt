ADJUSTED EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA is a measure of the Group’s operating performance and is calculated as net profit or loss for the 
Group before finance income and finance costs and tax, before any amount attributable to the amortisation of intangible 
assets and the depreciation of tangible assets and before any extraordinary items or the impairment of non-financial 
assets, bargain purchase gain and unrealised fair value loss on financial derivatives. A reconciliation of the Group’s 
adjusted EBITDA is provided in note 3 to the summarised financial statements.
