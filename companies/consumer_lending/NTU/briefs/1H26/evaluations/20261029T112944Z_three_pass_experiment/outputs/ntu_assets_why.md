## Section D003.10 | Purchased book debts (pp. 21–23)

### 1. Key facts and figures

**Instrument and measurement (p.21).** PBDs are acquired non-performing consumer/retail debt portfolios, classified **POCI (Stage 3) on initial recognition**; classification is irrevocable even if cash flows return to contractual terms. Measured at **amortised cost** using a rolling 12-month net cash-flow model discounted at a **credit-adjusted EIR**; weighted average rate **19.2%** (31 Mar 2025: 17.9%; 30 Sep 2025: 18.4%). Lifetime ECL changes go to P&L; favourable changes recognised as an **impairment gain** even below initial-recognition ECL. "Other financial assets" within PBD are at **fair value** (p.21).

### 2. Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **Sign convention.** The "Impairment (loss)/gain" column for the principal book is **positive and added** to gross to reach net — a cumulative POCI impairment gain, not a conventional ECL reserve. A decrease is an impairment loss. The p.23 column header "Expected credit loss allowance" is therefore misleading (added, not deducted); flagged, not corrected.
- **Mixed sign bases in one table (p.22).** Trade receivables impairment is a conventional **deduction** (−5 / −6 / −10), unlike the PBD gain-add convention. Do not net across columns.
- **Scope overlap.** p.22 "Total" (4,458 / 4,884) includes Other loans (5), Trade receivables (395), Other financial assets (644) — **not PBD alone**. PBD-only net = 3,845 (principal book). Do not sum p.22 and p.23 totals.
- **Retail anomaly.** Gain 51 > gross 46 → net 97 > gross; consistent with gain-add convention but unusual; header ambiguity preserved.
- **First-time presentation.** 31 Mar 2025 comparative "presented for the first time" (p.23) — not a restatement. The 31 Mar 2025 columns were also **represented** (split of gross vs impairment; other financial assets shown separately) — a presentation change, not a policy change.
- **Unresolved.** PBD roll-forward (opening, acquisitions, collections, amortisation, closing) not in this capture — Pending: D003.4 Note 1 (p.13). The R4,489m carrying value cited in Q-e4497d9112cd4e9d is **not** reconciled to 3,845 here. Whether the fair-value "Other financial assets" (644) sits inside the 3,845 principal book is unclear.
