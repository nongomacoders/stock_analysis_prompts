**Dictionary corrections (verified against source):**
- "Total" columns are not simple sums of all sub-columns. E.g. Barberton Mines total gold produced FY26 = 71,997 = 65,233 underground + 2,428 surface + 4,337 buy-in (excludes BTRP, which sits in Surface operations). Do not re-sum.
- "Tonnes milled and processed – total" mixes milled ore and processed tailings/feedstock; it is a combined throughput measure, not additive ore tonnage.
- FY25 Evander underground gold produced: 26,748 in underground column vs 27,829 in Evander Mines total — unresolved inconsistency (difference 1,081 = FY25 buy-in). Preserve as source inconsistency.
- FY25 Elikhulu all-in cost ZAR/kg printed 68,843 vs FY26 853,597 — anomalous; unresolved (possible typo).
- FY26 Group all-in cost printed "1,271 855" — spacing ambiguity; read as 1,271,855 ZAR/kg conditionally.
- Tennant A$ all-in cost FY25 1,584,835 vs FY26 205,143 — large swing, cause unknown.
