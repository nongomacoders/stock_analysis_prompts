### Executive Investment Thesis
Nutun is a deeply discounted special situation. The shares trade at R0.89 versus statutory NAV of R1.70 per share and tangible NAV ex-goodwill of approximately R0.96 per share. The market is pricing significant distress: operating cash flow was negative R111m in 1H26, the entire R3,845m principal PBD book is pledged, R1,396m of interest-bearing liabilities mature within 12 months, and only R475m of undrawn overdraft facilities is quantified. Management’s claim of approximately R0.8bn in unutilised facilities is not corroborated by the debt note.

The PBD book is purchased credit-impaired and permanently Stage 3. The R431m “ECL allowance” is a positive cumulative impairment gain, not conventional loss coverage. The reduction from R624m to R431m reflects deterioration in expected outperformance, driven by payer drop-offs and reduced settlement activity. The 12-month rolling ECL horizon is a methodological concern because lifetime tail risk is not disclosed. We therefore apply a 15% haircut to the PBD carrying value in our base-case SOTP.

The core earnings engine remains cash-generative before interest and reinvestment, but PBD amortisation must not be treated as a non-cash add-back. Portfolio replenishment is required to sustain collections. Our FCFE bridge explicitly subtracts PBD acquisitions and reflects net cash collections after reinvestment. We forecast FY26E core EBITDA of R1,548m, FY27E R1,620m, and FY28E R1,700m, but IFRS earnings remain weak: FY26E HEPS of -12.8c, FY27E -1.9c, and FY28E +9.6c.

The investment case hinges on refinancing the 12-month maturity wall, stabilising PBD collections, and executing cost reductions. If refinancing succeeds and PBD performance stabilises, the equity is materially undervalued. If refinancing fails or covenant headroom is insufficient, equity dilution or distress is likely. We rate NTU HOLD with a R0.95 target, offering 6.7% upside. A break above R1.00 would move the rating to Sell; a break below R0.83 would move it to Buy.

- PBD cumulative impairment gain fell from R624m to R431m, signalling weaker collection outperformance.

- Cost optimisation and headcount reductions in Nutun South Africa.
- AI and automation rollout expected to reduce payroll-to-revenue ratio and improve cost-to-serve.
- BPO billable seats grew 20% to 2,491; client base grew to 36.
- Potential asset sales or refinancing could unlock value.
- Deep discount to NAV provides margin of safety if liquidity risk is resolved.

## Section 6: Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

### Red Lines and Monitoring Triggers
- **Refinancing of R1,396m 12-month maturity wall:** Failure to refinance or extend by 30 September 2026 is a severe red line. Monitor SENS announcements for refinancing, covenant waivers, or new facilities.
- **Covenant headroom disclosure:** Management claims “comfortably complied” but provides no ratios. Any disclosure of LTV, interest cover, or net debt/EBITDA below covenant thresholds is a negative trigger.
- **Undrawn facilities:** Confirmed undrawn overdraft is R475m. A decline below R300m or reliance on short-term overdrafts to fund maturities is a red flag.
- **Operating cash flow:** 1H26 operating cash flow was negative R111m. A second consecutive half of negative operating cash flow would pressure liquidity.
- **PBD cumulative impairment gain:** Fell from R624m to R431m. A further decline below R300m would signal accelerating collection underperformance and potential PBD write-downs.
- **CAEIR vs market discount rate:** CAEIR 19.2% vs fair-value discount rate 18.50%. A widening gap or a rise in the market discount rate above CAEIR would increase fair-value risk.
- **PBD collections:** Monitor gross collections, net collections, and collection costs. A decline in net collections below R700m per half-year would impair cash generation.
- **H1 2026 audit/review status:** Results are unaudited and unreviewed. Any restatement or auditor review qualification is a material negative trigger.
- **FX sensitivity:** Rand strength pressures Nutun International margins. Monitor USD/ZAR and GBP/ZAR average rates.
- **NCA fee and rate caps:** No disclosure. Any regulatory action or cap tightening on collection economics is a risk.
- **Client concentration:** Top-10 concentration not disclosed. Loss of a major BPO or South African client would be negative.
- **AI execution:** Monitor payroll-to-revenue ratio and cost-to-serve. Failure to deliver AI-driven cost savings would undermine FY27E/FY28E margin assumptions.

### Catalysts
- Successful refinancing of the 12-month maturity wall on reasonable terms.
- Disclosure of covenant headroom and confirmation of the R0.8bn unutilised facilities claim.
- Stabilisation or improvement in PBD cumulative impairment gain.
- Accretive PBD acquisitions at healthy returns.
- Cost reduction and AI-driven efficiency gains in Nutun South Africa and Nutun International.
- Recovery in BPO margins as FX headwinds ease.
