## Section 2 — Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **POCI classification is explicit** (p.21): PBDs are Stage 3 POCI at initial recognition; lifetime ECL changes flow through P&L. This directly answers the measurement-basis question — the generic contractual-cash-flow ECL policy does **not** apply; a lifetime-ECL-since-initial-recognition model on amortised cost does.
- **"Impairment (loss)/gain" sign convention:** the table shows a **positive** 431/624/598 labelled "Purchased credit-impaired financial assets" and added to gross to reach net. This is a favourable lifetime-ECL movement (impairment gain), consistent with p.21's statement that favourable changes are recognised as gains. The label "allowance" in the concentration table is therefore a **signed gain**, not a reserve deduction — do not read it as a conventional ECL reserve.
- **Representation note (p.22):** the 31 Mar 2025 comparative has been **represented** to split gross carrying value from impairment for the principal book portfolio, and to show other financial assets separately. This is a presentation reclassification, not a restatement of measurement.
- **First-time disclosure note (p.23):** the concentration table was first presented in the FY2025 AFS; the 31 Mar 2025 comparative is presented for the first time. First-time comparative presentation alone does not establish a policy change.
- **Scope mismatch to flag:** the p.22 "Principal book portfolio" gross (3,414) and net (3,845) tie to the p.23 concentration table, but the p.21 narrative refers to "PBDs" and "other financial assets recognised within the purchase book debts" (644 at Mar 2026) measured at fair value — so the PBD carrying value in the statement of financial position may differ from the 3,845 principal-book figure. The R4,489m carrying value referenced in stored questions is **not visible in this capture**; reconcile scope before comparing.
- **EIR trend:** 17.9% → 18.4% → 19.2% (Mar 2025 → Sep 2025 → Mar 2026), a rising discount rate that increases amortisation/impairment sensitivity; no driver split disclosed here.
- **No roll-forward** (opening, acquisitions, collections, amortisation, closing) is presented in this capture — only point-in-time balances.
