## 2. Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **POCI classification is explicit and permanent** (p.21): PBDs are Stage 3 POCI on initial recognition; classification does not change even if cash flows return to contractual terms. Lifetime ECL changes recognised in P&L; favourable changes recognised as impairment gain even if lifetime ECLs fall below the amount embedded at initial recognition. This directly answers the measurement-basis question (Q-08aa5c3f257440ab).
- **Valuation method:** monthly modelling of net cash flows over a **rolling 12-month period**, discounted at a credit-adjusted EIR to amortised cost. "Other financial assets" within PBD include a receivables balance at **fair value** (PV of future cash flows net of costs) — a mixed-measurement element inside the PBD caption.
- **Representation footnote (p.22):** the 31 Mar 2025 comparative was **represented** to split gross carrying value vs impairment for the principal book, and to show other financial assets separately. This is a **presentation reclassification**, not a restatement of measurement — do not read it as a policy change or prior-period error.
- **First-time presentation footnote (p.23):** the concentration table was first presented in the FY2025 AFS; the 31 Mar 2025 comparative is presented for the first time. First-time comparative ≠ restatement.
- **Unresolved scope difference:** the p.22 "Principal book portfolio" gross (3,414) and the p.23 concentration total gross (3,414) agree, but the p.22 caption "Other financial assets" (644) is separate from the principal book; the p.21 text says other financial assets are "recognised within the purchase book debts." Whether the R644m sits inside or outside the R3,845m principal-book carrying value is **not reconciled** in this capture — leave open.
- **No roll-forward** (opening, acquisitions, collections, amortisation, closing) is present in this capture; the R4,489m carrying value referenced in stored questions is **not visible here** (this capture shows R3,845m principal book / R4,884m total). Do not force a reconciliation.
