**Measurement bases (p.13):**
- **Principal book portfolios:** written-off consumer debts bought at deep discount; **amortised cost** on a portfolio basis; cost = vendor price + direct acquisition costs; carrying amount includes cumulative lifetime ECL as a loss allowance; ECL changes run through P&L as impairment gain/loss. Favourable ECL changes recognised as impairment gain even where lifetime ECL is below acquisition-date expected credit losses (i.e. POCI-style mechanics).
- **Other financial assets:** purchased book debt contracts where Nutun **does not hold title**; non-performing accounts held by SA banks/credit providers; return driven by collections; **no recourse** to banks/providers; titleholder may retract accounts and sell to third parties (Nutun entitled to a share of proceeds). Fails SPPI → **fair value** measurement (cross-ref note 6.3).
