### Candidate reusable rule for explicit approval

- **Code/sector:** `CONSUMER-LENDING-TRADE-RECEIVABLES-SIMPLIFIED-ECL`, consumer_lending, section/Stage1/Stage2 applicability.
- **Title:** Establish the ECL model before alleging a staging defect.
- **Mandate:** Classify the instrument and establish whether simplified lifetime ECL is mandatory or elected. For IFRS15 trade receivables without a significant financing component, use the required lifetime approach; for eligible receivables with a significant financing component, verify the entity's policy choice. Where simplified lifetime ECL applies, do not demand general-model Stage1/2 migrations or a 30-DPD SICR backstop. Evaluate provision-matrix losses using historical/current/forward-looking evidence. Zero ELRs alone prove neither adequacy nor manipulation. If policy applicability is uncertain, record the precise uncertainty rather than invent a staging breach.
- **Confirmed failure/trigger:** C013; a service/trade receivable provision matrix is being interpreted through loan staging. Matrix presentation is a trigger to check policy, not conclusive proof of election.
- **Required evidence:** Instrument/transaction type, terms/financing component, policy where choice exists, aging/matrix and loss experience/forward adjustments; physical-page citations.
- **Exceptions:** General-model instruments, POCI and other eligible policy choices must be assessed under their own requirements; do not relabel purchased portfolios as trade-service receivables.
- **Double-counting risk:** Do not add an arbitrary credit haircut on top of an already modeled expected cash loss or add loss allowances twice.
- **Should fail:** “0% at31 days proves failure to transfer to Stage2” where simplified lifetime ECL is established.
- **Should pass:** “Lifetime ECL policy established; no SICR-stage test; zero matrix rates require portfolio-specific support, not automatic acceptance.”

No same-code rule existed in the inspected production snapshot. This candidate must complement POCI classification, not override it or the withholding gate.
