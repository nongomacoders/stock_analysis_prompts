## 1. Executive Forensic Audit Summary

Nutun is not a conventional originator lender. **Nutun South Africa** is a principal purchaser/collector of unsecured NPL/PBD portfolios and an agent for third-party clients. **Nutun International** is a BPO/customer-engagement business. No separate corporate-services segment is disclosed; group-level costs/tax/finance sit in the group column. Therefore originator NIM/CLR, general-model Stage 1/2/3 migration and ABS terms are not applicable unless separately established.

The principal PBD portfolio is **POCI at amortised cost**. At 31 March 2026: gross carrying R3,414m + cumulative impairment gain R431m = net carrying R3,845m. The R431m is a **favourable cumulative ECL gain**, not a conventional loss allowance; 431/3,414 is not loss coverage. The period impairment loss of R167m reduces the cumulative gain from R598m to R431m. The net carrying bridge reconciles: 3,975 + 382 − 728 + 383 − 167 = 3,845. The other FVTPL PBD portfolio is separate and reconciles: 702 + 28 − 116 + 30 = 644. Total PBD carrying R4,489m reconciles: 4,677 + 410 − 844 + 383 − 167 + 30 = 4,489.

Historical-book impairment losses are six-month movements: H1 FY26 R191m; H1 FY25 R77m; H2 FY25 R182m. Full FY25 historical-book loss = 77 + 182 = R259m. Current H1 is not a full-year result. Vintage/FX attribution is not disclosed.

Liquidity evidence: cash R112m + R1m held-for-sale cash = R113m; undrawn overdraft facilities R475m; management states ~R0.8bn unutilised facilities; interest-bearing liabilities R4,016m, with R1,396m payable within 12 months and R2,620m thereafter. PBD R3,845m is pledged. A bank renewal/revolve commitment to 30 September 2027 is referenced in the Mobalyz settlement footnote. Directors’ going-concern statement is management evidence, not assurance. No dated funding shortfall is established from static maturity-minus-cash arithmetic.

Statutory cash-flow arithmetic reconciles: operating −111, investing −61, financing +178 = +6; opening cash 107 + 6 = 113; balance-sheet cash 112 + HFS 1 = 113. NAV = 1,329 / 780.651033m = **R1.70/share**; share capital R5,410m is not NAV. Basic LPS = −63 / 782.1m = −8.06c, printed −8.1c. PBT −92; tax credit +29; tax expense −29; conventional ETR = (−29)/(−92) = **31.5% credit**.

Material unresolved items for Stage 2 are facility-level covenant/restricted-cash detail, tax/DTA support, and forward PBD collection/impairment assumptions. The note-level PBD bridges are verified; the statutory cash-flow line “Purchased book debts R182m” is not like-for-like with note net collections R844m and cannot be reconciled without a full indirect-method bridge.

## 3. Audit of Common Surface-Reading Pitfalls

- **Pitfall:** Treating R431m cumulative impairment gain as loss coverage or “coverage collapse.” **Correction:** It is a favourable cumulative ECL gain; 431/3,414 is not coverage.
- **Pitfall:** Comparing net collections R844m directly with carrying-value change −188m or statutory cash-flow line R182m. **Correction:** Include additions, CAEIR, impairment and FV movements. Note-level bridge reconciles; statutory line is not like-for-like.
- **Pitfall:** Calling zero trade ECL rates a staging defect. **Correction:** Simplified lifetime ECL policy is not established; no general-model staging applies. Zero rates require support, not automatic manipulation.
- **Pitfall:** Treating H1 FY26 historical-book loss R191m as full-year. **Correction:** It is six months; FY25 was 77 + 182 = 259.
- **Pitfall:** Treating ~R0.8bn unutilised facilities as undrawn overdrafts. **Correction:** Undrawn overdrafts are R475m; broader facilities ~R0.8bn. Static subtraction is not a dated shortfall.
- **Pitfall:** Treating share capital R5,410m as NAV. **Correction:** NAV is R1,329m / 780.651m = R1.70/share.
- **Pitfall:** Treating tax credit R29m as positive ETR. **Correction:** Tax expense is −29; ETR is 31.5% credit.
- **Pitfall:** Treating going-concern statement as assurance. **Correction:** It is management evidence; statements are unaudited and not reviewed.
- **Pitfall:** Treating missing issuer forecast as defect. **Correction:** Analyst assumptions are permitted; unsupported material inputs still block valuation.

## Stage 2 Handoff: Unresolved Material Dependencies

Stage 2 must **withhold valuation** while a material dependency remains unresolved. Do not declare a 99% confidence baseline. Do not publish a target, rating, upside, dividend yield or recommendation.

1. **Facility-level liquidity and covenant headroom — NOT VERIFIED.**  
   Evidence: pp.5, 14–15, 35–36. We have undrawn overdrafts R475m, unutilised facilities ~R0.8bn, maturity R1,396m within 12 months, bank renewal commitment to 30 Sep 2027, and management going-concern statement. Missing: facility-by-facility maturities, covenant calculations/headroom, restricted/pledged cash breakdown, conditions and renewal terms. Consequence: liquidity runway cannot be independently verified; Stage 2 must use explicit supported assumptions and sensitivities or withhold valuation.

2. **Tax/DTA assumptions — NOT VERIFIED.**  
   Evidence: pp.8–9. ETR computed as 31.5% credit; DTA R173m, DTL R513m. Missing: tax note, DTA recoverability, tax-loss/assessed-loss support. Consequence: sustainable tax rate and deferred-tax cash effects cannot be verified; Stage 2 must disclose assumptions or withhold.

3. **Forward PBD collection/impairment assumptions — ASSUMPTION REQUIRED.**  
   Evidence: pp.13, 21–24, 31. Historical bridges reconcile. Missing: forward collection curves by portfolio/vintage, vintage/FX split, and management’s forward macro assumptions. Consequence: Stage 2 may use explicit analyst assumptions with sensitivities; unsupported material inputs prevent publication.

4. **Statutory PBD cash-flow reconciliation — PRESENTATION ITEM, NOT A COMPANY DEFECT.**  
   Evidence: p.12 line “Purchased book debts 182” vs note net collections 844. Difference 662 is not a like-for-like residual because the cash-flow line is an indirect-method working-capital adjustment. Missing: full indirect-method bridge. Consequence: use note-level cash collections for modelling; do not treat difference as error.

5. **Trade receivables ECL policy — IMMATERIAL / NON-BLOCKER.**  
   Evidence: pp.20, 22, 25. Net financial bridge reconciles; allowance R5m. Policy/financing component not fully established. Consequence: not material to valuation; no staging allegation.

6. **Debt/lease residuals — IMMATERIAL.**  
   Evidence: pp.8, 12, 14–15. Interest-bearing residual −3m; lease residual +11m. Consequence: not a material blocker; evidence needed for non-cash/FX/lease additions if modelling debt precisely.

**Stage 1 verdict:** Historical PBD, cash-flow, equity and EPS junctions are substantially verified. Material Stage 2 blockers remain liquidity/covenant detail and tax/DTA support; forward PBD assumptions must be explicit and sensitised. No valuation or recommendation is issued.
