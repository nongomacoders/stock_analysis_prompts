It has two economic engines: **Nutun South Africa** — principal PBD/RTC purchaser and agency collector of unsecured NPLs — and **Nutun International** — BPO/customer-engagement and collections services. Originator NIM/CLR, general-model Stage-1/2/3 staging for the POCI principal book, and ABS terms are therefore **not applicable unless specifically established**. Relevant Nutun metrics are PBD gross/net collections, acquisitions, CAEIR, signed ECL adjustments, ERC, FVTPL movements, cash conversion, facility headroom and segment EBITDA.

**H1 2026 reported economics.** Statutory revenue was **R711m** versus R745m; IFRS operating loss **R(79)m**; loss before tax **R(92)m**; income tax credit **+R29m**; continuing and total loss **R(63)m**. Core continuing loss also **R(63)m**, after nil exceptional adjustments in H1 2026; H1 2025 core loss R(71)m reconciles from IFRS continuing loss R(122)m plus R51m exceptional items. Core EBITDA was **R790m**, but this is a non-IFRS CODM measure, not free cash flow.

**Balance-sheet core.** PBD/RTC carrying value is **R4,489m**, comprising principal POCI **R3,845m** (gross R3,414m plus cumulative impairment gain R431m) and other FVTPL purchased book debts **R644m**. ERC is **R8,366m**. Principal roll ties: **3,975 + 382 additions − 728 net collections + 383 CAEIR − 167 impairment = 3,845**. Other FVTPL roll ties: **702 + 28 − 116 + 30 = 644**. The R431m positive cumulative balance is **not conventional loss coverage**; it is a cumulative impairment gain. The R167m current impairment loss is a six-month movement, not an annual figure.

**Liquidity.** Cash **R112m**, bank overdrafts **R273m**, simple net **R(161)m**. Interest-bearing debt excluding overdrafts **R3,743m**; total interest-bearing liabilities including overdrafts **R4,016m**; lease liabilities **R345m**. Maturity within 12 months **R1,396m**. Only **R475m undrawn overdraft facilities** are explicitly disclosed; management refers to approximately **R0.8bn unutilised facilities** but no full facility schedule, covenant calculations, restricted-cash amount, conditions or dated cash forecast is supplied. A static maturity-minus-cash calculation is not a dated funding shortfall.

**Verdict.** The historical PBD reconciliations, share/NAV arithmetic, core reconciliations and cash-flow totals are substantially substantiated. The material unresolved issue is forward liquidity/covenant support. No target, rating, upside or valuation is produced. R0.89 is not used; it is a frozen comparison reference only.

## 2. Detailed Forensic Reasoning & Determinations

### 2.1 PBD — principal POCI and other FVTPL

The principal book is purchased credit-impaired at acquisition and remains Stage 3/POCI; it is not subsequently managed by credit-risk rating but on a cash-collection basis. Other financial assets are purchased debt contracts without title, non-SPPI and measured at FVTPL. They must not be blended into one ECL regime.

**Principal POCI reconciliation, 30 Sep 2025 to 31 Mar 2026 (six months):**  
3,975 + 382 − 728 + 383 − 167 = **3,845**.  
Net collections: gross **773** less directly attributable collection costs **45** = **728**.  
The “impairment loss” is **R(167)m**; the cumulative balance fell from **598** to **431**. This is a signed cumulative impairment gain, not a loss allowance divided by gross carrying amount. The R431m is not a coverage ratio.

**Other FVTPL:** 702 + 28 − 116 + 30 = **644**. This is a separate FVTPL portfolio; its cash collections and FV movements are not POCI ECL. Combined additions were **382 + 28 = 410**, matching the R410m acquisition cost on p.3.

**Statutory cash-flow comparison.** The separate portfolio reconciliations tie. However, the statutory cash-flow line “decrease in operating assets — purchased book debts” is **R182m**, while combined carrying value moved from R4,677m to R4,489m, a **R188m decrease**. These are not like-for-like: the cash-flow line is an indirect-method working-capital movement that already sits alongside non-cash CAEIR, impairment, FV and other operating adjustments. The apparent R370m difference is not a clean residual and is left unexplained pending a full indirect-method bridge. No FX or rounding plug is invented.

**PBD amortisation.** The printed non-IFRS amortisation charge is **R597m**. The p.31 footnote lists CAEIR R383m, impairment R167m, net collections R728m, FV movements R30m and other cash collections R116m. The prose lists components without explicit operators; this is not, by itself, a defect. The exact signed arithmetic bridge to R597m is **not verified**, but the disclosed portfolio reconciliations support the underlying components. A one-arrangement calculation gives R598m, i.e. R1m rounding proximity, but because operators are not explicit it is not asserted as a formal bridge. Consequence: use R597m as reported historical APM; Stage 2 must model its own signed PBD amortisation formula.

### 2.2 ECL migration and allowance rollforward

Principal PBD is POCI/Stage 3 from inception; no Stage-1/2 SICR migration applies. Other FVTPL assets do not use ECL. Trade receivables have standard 30-day terms; the note uses aging, past default experience and forward-looking conditions, but does not explicitly establish simplified lifetime ECL policy or a significant-financing-component conclusion. Therefore do not demand general-model staging or allege SICR failure from zero matrix rates.

Principal-book ECL allowance rollforward read correctly as periods:  
- H1 FY25: total **R(173)m** = acquired **R(96)m** loss + historical **R(77)m** loss.  
- H2 FY25: total **R(26)m** loss = acquired **+R156m** gain + historical **R(182)m** loss.  
- FY25 total **R(199)m** loss; FY25 historical loss **R(259)m**.  
- H1 FY26: total **R(167)m** loss = acquired **+R24m** gain + historical **R(191)m** loss.  
The current R191m historical loss is a **six-month** movement; do not annualise it by default. Management attributes the reduction in the cumulative gain to payer drop-offs/reduced settlement activity.

### 2.3 Trade and other receivables; simplification before allegation

Balance-sheet trade and other receivables are **R497m**. The net financial receivables bridge on p.22 is: trade and other receivables gross **R395m**, impairment **R(5)m**, carrying **R390m**. The difference to the R497m statutory line is **R107m**, explicitly excluded as prepayments, VAT and deposits (non-financial). This bridges the statutory line without treating it as a receivable-ECL defect.

The trade-receivables matrix on p.25 shows gross **R374m**, allowance **R(5)m**, net **R369m**, total ELR **1.34%**; current/30-day/61–90 ELRs are 0.00%, 90+ 100%. The R21m difference between p.25 trade receivables gross R374m and p.22 trade/other gross R395m is other receivables/classification detail not separately explained; it is small and not a material ECL defect. Zero ELRs at short maturities prove neither adequacy nor manipulation. The simplified lifetime ECL policy is not explicit; record uncertainty, not a staging breach.

The core-EBITDA reconciliation labels “Tax expense” as **R(29)m** because it removes the credit from after-tax loss; this is a sign convention, not a contradiction.

Closing issued shares: **784,313,242**; treasury **3,662,209**; net outstanding **780,651,033**. Parent ordinary equity **R1,329m**. Statutory NAV/share = **1,329 / 780.651 = R1.70** approximately. ### 2.5 Liquidity, debt, covenants and going concern

Cash-flow closing cash **R113m** includes **R1m** HFS cash; balance-sheet cash **R112m**, so statutory cash + HFS cash reconciles. Overdrafts move from R364m to R273m, consistent with net overdraft settlement **R91m**. No covenant ratios, restricted cash, conditions or dated cash-flow forecast are disclosed. Directors’ going-concern statement is management evidence, not assurance. Static maturity minus cash/facilities is not a dated forecast funding shortfall.

### 2.6 Revenue, segments and core reconciliation

p.36 geographical total revenue **R1,599m** reconciles exactly to statutory revenue **R711m** plus PBD CODM revenue **R888m**: **711 + 888 = 1,599**. The PBD CODM revenue is eliminated in IFRS. H1 statutory revenue declined R34m; geography reconciles the decline (SA −29, Australia −47, UK +44, US −12, Other +10), but FX/constant-currency and volume/price/client drivers are not fully bridged. p.4 gives adverse average FX R27m; no full constant-currency bridge. This is a forecast-assumption matter, not a historical arithmetic failure.

Core reconciliation is substantiated for disclosed periods: H1 2026 core loss equals IFRS loss R(63)m with exceptional items nil; H1 2025 IFRS R(122)m + R51m exceptional = R(71)m; FY25 IFRS R(113)m + R68m = R(45)m. Put/call and other policy categories are not separately listed as nil, but no contrary evidence is present.

### 2.7 NCA, securitisation and ABS

NCA fee/rate caps are not quantified and no breach evidence is provided. Nutun South Africa is a purchaser/collector, not the original lender; NCA cap analysis requires instrument-specific evidence. Securitisation/ABS terms are not established; the funding note shows secured notes, loans and a revolving facility, not an ABS structure. Do not demand ABS terms absent applicability.

| Naive reading | Correct forensic treatment |
|---|---|
| “R431m impairment allowance means coverage” | It is a **positive cumulative impairment gain**, not a loss allowance. Do not calculate 431/3,414 as coverage. |
| “PBD fair value exceeds carrying / R167m impairment is annual” | R167m is a **six-month** P&L loss; the R431m balance is cumulative. The current loss reduced the cumulative gain. |
| “Net collections R728m versus carrying decline R130m is wrong” | Must include additions R382m, CAEIR R383m, impairment R167m and period opening/closing. The principal roll ties exactly. |
| “Zero trade-receivable ELR proves under-provision or Stage-2 failure” | Simplified lifetime ECL applicability/policy is not explicitly established; zero matrix rates alone prove neither adequacy nor manipulation. |
| “Undrawn overdrafts R475m = total liquidity” | Only overdrafts are disclosed; management refers to ~R0.8bn unutilised facilities. No full schedule or covenant headroom. |
| “Within-12-month debt R1,396m minus cash is a funding hole” | That is a static stock subtraction, not a dated cash forecast. Collections, acquisitions, expenses, security, renewals and conditions are required. |
| “Directors say going concern, so audited assurance” | It is management evidence, not external assurance. |
| “Use 782.1m weighted shares for NAV” | Use closing net shares **780.651m** for NAV; weighted average 782.1m is for EPS. |
| “Tax expense is +R29m” | P&L shows an income tax **credit** of R29m; core-EBITDA reconciliation uses R(29)m to remove the credit. |

## 5. Stage 2 Handoff — Unresolved Material Dependencies

1. **Funding, liquidity and covenant headroom — NOT VERIFIED (material).**  
   Evidence: p.5 ~R0.8bn unutilised facilities; p.14 undrawn overdrafts R475m, total interest-bearing liabilities R4,016m, within-12-month maturity R1,396m; p.35 historical bank-funding renewal commitment.  
   Missing evidence: facility-by-facility limits/drawn/undrawn, maturity dates, covenant definitions and compliance calculations, restricted/pledged cash amounts, conditions, security, renewal terms, and a dated cash forecast incorporating collections, PBD/RTC purchases, expenses, tax, capex and financing.  
   Consequence: no dated funding runway or covenant-headroom conclusion. Stage 2 must withhold valuation unless it builds an explicit supported liquidity/covenant forecast and sensitivities.

2. **PBD forecast and amortisation methodology — NOT FULLY VERIFIED (material model dependency).**  
   Evidence: p.13 principal roll and FVTPL roll tie; p.21 CAEIR 19.2%, rolling 12-month net cash flow methodology; p.31 R597m component list; ERC R8,366m.  
   Missing evidence: explicit signed amortisation formula, portfolio-level ERC basis, collection curves/tail methodology, collection-cost assumptions, and sensitivity to CAEIR/market discount rates.  
   Consequence: historical portfolio balances are substantiated; forecast PBD revenue, amortisation and cash conversion require explicit analyst assumptions and sensitivities. If these cannot be supported, withhold valuation.

**Immaterial / non-blockers:** R3m debt and R11m lease unexplained residuals; R21m trade-receivable classification difference; zero short-dated ELR on trade receivables; lack of ABS terms; lack of originator NIM/CLR; directors’ going-concern statement lacking quantitative support. These are either immaterial, non-applicable, or management evidence rather than assurance.

**Stage 1 verdict:** historical audit largely substantiated; forward valuation **WITHHELD — MATERIAL LIQUIDITY/COVENANT AND PBD FORECAST DEPENDENCIES UNRESOLVED**. No target, rating, upside, yield or recommendation is produced.
