## D003.10 — Purchased book debts: accounting meaning (provisional)

**Activity/instrument.** Nutun acquires non-performing consumer/retail debt portfolios (PBDs) from credit providers, then collects via analytics-driven processes. Instrument = purchased credit-impaired (POCI) financial assets, **Stage 3 on initial recognition**; classification does not change even if cash flows return to contractual terms (p.21).

**Measurement basis.** Amortised cost via monthly modelling: net cash flows over a rolling 12-month period, discounted at a **credit-adjusted effective interest rate**; weighted average rate **19.2%** (31 Mar 2025: 17.9%; 30 Sep 2025: 18.4%) (p.21). Changes in lifetime ECLs go to P&L; favourable changes recognised as an **impairment gain** even if lifetime ECLs fall below initial-recognition ECLs (p.21). "Other financial assets" within PBD are at **fair value** (p.21).

**Sign convention (critical).** In the p.22 table, "Impairment (loss)/gain" for the principal book is **positive** (431 / 624 / 598) and is *added* to gross carrying value to reach net. Per the sector note and p.21, this is a **cumulative POCI impairment gain**, not a reserve: a decrease is an impairment loss, not a release. Do not read it as a conventional ECL allowance.
