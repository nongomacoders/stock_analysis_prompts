**D009 Group production summary — provisional accounting meanings (pp. 48–50)**

**Scope/period:** Gold mining group; FY26 vs FY25 (years ended 30 June 2026/2025). Segments: Barberton Mines, Evander Mines, surface operations (BTRP, Elikhulu, MTR), Tennant Mines (Australia). Units: tonnes (t), g/t, %, oz, ZAR/kg, A$/kg, US$/oz, ZAR/t, US$m, ZARm, A$m. FX: US$/ZAR 16.90 (FY26)/18.17 (FY25); US$/A$ 1.47/1.54.

**Terms (meaning in this disclosure):**
- **Tonnes milled – underground/surface:** ore milled at own mines (t). **Tonnes processed – tailings/surface feedstock/third-party buy-in:** material treated at surface operations; buy-in is purchased, not own, material — kept in separate columns.
- **Head grade – total vs excluding third-party buy-in:** g/t; the "excluding" line strips purchased feedstock, so it is not comparable to "total."
- **Overall recovered grade / recovery:** g/t and %; recovery = recovered metal ÷ feed, by circuit (underground vs tailings).
- **Gold produced – total:** oz, split underground/surface/tailings/buy-in/surface feedstock; **Gold sold – total:** oz (differs from produced; inventory timing).
- **Average gold price received:** ZAR/kg (SA ops), A$/kg (Australian ops), US$/oz (Group) — realized prices, not spot.
- **ZAR cash cost / AISC / all-in cost:** ZAR/kg (and A$/kg, US$/oz equivalents); AISC adds sustaining capex to cash cost; all-in adds further. **US$ AISC – excluding third-party buy-in feedstock:** separate basis, not comparable to headline AISC.
- **ZAR cash cost per tonne:** ZAR/t.
- **Capital expenditure:** US$m. **Revenue / Cost of production / AISC of production / All-in cost of production:** ZARm and A$m. **Adjusted EBITDA:** US$m (APM; definition pending D008).

**Signs:** Costs and capex are outflows (reduce profit/assets); revenue and EBITDA inflows. No sign conventions stated beyond labels.

**Ambiguities/overlaps:** "Total" columns aggregate sub-columns (e.g., Barberton = Mines + BTRP + buy-in); do not re-sum.
