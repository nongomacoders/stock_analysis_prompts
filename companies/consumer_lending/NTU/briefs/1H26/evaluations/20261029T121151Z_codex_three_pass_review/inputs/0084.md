## Section 1 — Key facts and figures

**Instrument & basis (p.21):** PBDs are **POCI financial assets, Stage 3 on initial recognition**; classification does not change even if cash flows return to full contractual terms. Changes in **lifetime ECLs** go to P&L; favourable changes are recognised as an **impairment gain** even if lifetime ECLs fall below the amount embedded at initial recognition. Valued monthly at **amortised cost** using a **credit-adjusted EIR**; weighted average credit-adjusted EIR **19.2%** (31 Mar 2025: 17.9%; 30 Sep 2025: 18.4%). "Other financial assets" within PBD include a receivables balance at **fair value**.

**Principal book portfolio (Rm, p.22):**

|---|---|---|---|
| Gross carrying value | 3,414 | 3,066 | 3,377 |
| POCI impairment gain | 431 | 624 | 598 |
| Carrying value | 3,845 | 3,690 | 3,975 |

Total financial assets subject to risk: **4,884** (Mar 2026) vs 4,859 (Mar 2025) vs 5,059 (Sep 2025).

**Concentration (p.23, Rm gross / ECL / net):** Banking & specialised lending – unsecured 2,371 / 227 / 2,598; Debt review 951 / 141 / 1,092; Retail 46 / 51 / 97; Banking – secured 46 / 12 / 58. Totals 3,414 / 431 / 3,845.

**Arithmetic checks:** 3,414 + 431 = 3,845 ✓; 3,066 + 624 = 3,690 ✓; 3,377 + 598 = 3,975 ✓. Concentration rows sum to totals ✓ (Mar 2026: 2,371+951+46+46 = 3,414; 227+141+51+12 = 431). Note the **sign convention**: the "impairment gain" is *added* to gross to reach carrying value — i.e. it is a cumulative POCI gain embedded in the book, not a reserve deducted. A **decrease** in this balance (624→431, −193) is therefore an **impairment loss**, not a reserve release.

**Movement H1 FY26:** gross +37 (3,377→3,414); cumulative POCI gain −167 (598→431); net carrying −130 (3,975→3,845). YoY: gross +348; gain −193; net +155.

## Section 2 — Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **Representation, not restatement (p.22):** 31 Mar 2025 comparatives were **represented** to split gross carrying value from impairment and to show other financial assets separately. This is a presentation change, not evidence of prior error.
- **First-time disclosure (p.23):** the concentration table was first presented in the FY2025 AFS; the 31 Mar 2025 comparative is presented for the first time. Absence in earlier interim reports is not a defect.
- **Mixed measurement within PBD:** the note says PBDs are POCI at amortised cost, but "other financial assets" inside the PBD caption are at **fair value**. The R644m (Mar 2026) / R663m / R702m "other financial assets" column is therefore not POCI amortised cost — scope must be separated before comparing to the R3,845m principal book.
- **Unresolved sign/definition tension:** the table labels the 431/624/598 line "Impairment (loss)/gain" and separately "Purchased credit-impaired financial assets," added to gross. The narrative calls favourable lifetime-ECL changes an impairment gain. Whether the 431 is a cumulative gain embedded at acquisition or a cumulative post-acquisition ECL movement is **not stated** — leave open.
- **Retail anomaly:** Retail ECL (51) exceeds gross (46) at Mar 2026, giving net 97 > gross — consistent with a cumulative gain added, but the label "expected credit loss allowance" is then misleading. Flag, do not resolve.
- **No roll-forward** (opening, acquisitions, collections, amortisation, closing) is given in this capture; the movement above is derived from balance-sheet points only.
