Sector notes for the shared section-reading prompt:

Consumer lending and credit services:distinguish loan origination,purchased debt portfolios and fee/BPO services. Establish instrument and measurement basis before applying staging,coverage,NIM,rate-cap or securitisation checks. Read collections,portfolio movements,credit losses and funding only where relevant; do not treat signed POCI gains or FVTPL changes as conventional ECL reserves. Where the issuer adds a cumulative POCI impairment gain to the book value, a decrease is an impairment loss, not a reserve release; distinguish acquired-book gains from historical-book losses.

For POCI assets, assess changes in lifetime expected losses since initial recognition; do not request ordinary Stage 1/2 SICR migration tables. Still examine relevant recovery assumptions, signed measurement reconciliations and the issuer's disclosed credit-risk classifications. Do not turn this distinction into a ban on legitimate POCI credit-risk questions.
