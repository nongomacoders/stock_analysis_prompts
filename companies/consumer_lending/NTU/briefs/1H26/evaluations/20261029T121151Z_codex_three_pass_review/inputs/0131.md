## Section 2 — Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **No footnotes on pp. 48–50.** APM definitions (AISC, all-in, Adjusted EBITDA) are not in this section — Pending: D008.
- **"Total" columns are not sums of all sub-columns.** Barberton Mines total gold produced FY26 = 71,997 = 65,233 underground + 2,428 surface + 4,337 buy-in (BTRP sits under Surface operations, not Barberton). Do not re-sum.
- **Group produced FY26 check:** 105,478 + 2,428 + 113,613 + 18,667 + 32,124 = 272,310 ✓ (underground column prints 105,479; 1 oz rounding residual).
- **Group produced FY25 check:** 93,724 + 2,654 + 98,636 + 1,081 + 1,513 = 197,608 ≠ 196,527 printed. Difference 1,081 = FY25 buy-in oz — suggests buy-in may be embedded in another column in FY25. Unresolved; do not plug.
- **FY25 Evander underground gold produced:** 26,748 in underground column vs 27,829 in Evander Mines total (difference 1,081 = FY25 buy-in). Source inconsistency; unresolved.
- **FY25 Elikhulu all-in cost ZAR/kg printed 68,843** vs FY26 853,597 — anomalous (likely typo); unresolved.
- **FY26 Group all-in cost printed "1,271 855"** — spacing ambiguity; read conditionally as 1,271,855 ZAR/kg.
- **Tennant A$ all-in cost FY25 1,584,835 → FY26 205,143** — large swing on a tiny base; cause not established.
- **Produced vs sold:** FY26 +63 oz, FY25 +399 oz — inventory timing, not explained here.
- **"Excluding third-party buy-in" AISC** is a separate basis, not comparable to headline AISC.
- **Revenue/EBITDA/capex arithmetic:** Revenue 8,449.6 + 8,799.5 = 17,249.1 ✓; CoP 3,957.4 + 2,474.3 = 6,431.7 ✓; Adj EBITDA 263.4 + 399.7 = 663.1 ✓; Capex 87.3 + 127.6 = 214.9 ✓.
