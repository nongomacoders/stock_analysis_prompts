## 1. Executive Forensic Audit Summary

Nutun is not a conventional originator consumer lender. The evidence classifies it as: **Nutun South Africa** — principal purchaser of unsecured NPL portfolios, with a separate agent collections/recovery business; and **Nutun International** — BPO/customer engagement and collections/recovery services for UK/US/Australia clients. Corporate services are not a separate lending portfolio. Therefore originator NIM/CLR, general-model staging and ABS terms are not applied absent evidence. **R0.89 is a frozen reference only; no current quote or subsequent SENS is provided, so it is not used.**

The 1H26 statutory result is a **R63m loss**; NAV is **R1,329m**, or **R1.70/share** on 780.651m closing shares. The principal POCI portfolio reconciles arithmetically: gross R3,414m + cumulative impairment gain R431m = net R3,845m; opening net R3,975m + additions R382m − net collections R728m + CAEIR R383m − impairment R167m = R3,845m. Other FVTPL purchased book debt separately reconciles: R702m + R28m − R116m + R30m = R644m. The R431m “expected credit loss allowance” is a **signed cumulative impairment gain**, not conventional loss coverage; 431/3,414 is not coverage.

Liquidity is the key unresolved area. Management states approximately **R0.8bn unutilised facilities**, but the note only quantifies **R475m undrawn overdraft facilities**; R1,396m of interest-bearing liabilities mature within 12 months. A static maturity-minus-cash/undrawn-overdraft calculation is **not** a dated funding forecast and is not used as one. Directors’ going-concern statement is management evidence, not assurance; the half-year information is unreviewed by auditors.

## 2. Detailed Forensic Reasoning & Determinations

### 2.1 Portfolio classification and separate reconciliations

**Principal POCI/amortised-cost portfolio.** Policy: purchased credit-impaired at initial recognition, classified Stage 3, not reclassified even if cash flows recover; lifetime ECL changes recognised in P/L, including favourable impairment gains (pp.21,24). Net carrying reconciles as above (pp.13,22–24). Gross carrying R3,414m; cumulative impairment gain R431m; net R3,845m. The R167m current-period impairment loss comprises acquired-portfolio gain R24m less historical-portfolio loss R191m. No conventional coverage or “coverage collapse” inference is made. The rolling 12-month cash-flow modelling and lifetime/tail methodology are **not fully evidenced**; missing methodology is NOT VERIFIED, not proof of misstatement.

**Other FVTPL purchased book debt.** Separate from POCI: no title/SPPI, managed on fair-value basis. R702m + additions R28m − cash collections R116m + FV gains R30m = R644m (pp.28–29). Discount rate is 16% (Mar-25 12.93%; Sep-25 16%). No ECL staging is applied to this portfolio.

**Combined PBD balance.** Opening R4,677m + additions R410m + CAEIR R383m + FV R30m − impairment R167m − net cash collections R844m = closing R4,489m. Net carrying decrease is R188m. The statutory cash-flow statement shows “Decrease in operating assets – purchased book debts” R182m (p.12). Component reconciliation leaves an **unexplained R6m like-for-like residual** between the net carrying decrease and that cash-flow line. The non-IFRS PBD amortisation sum reconstructs to R598m versus printed R597m, a R1m rounding residual. These are not plugged; they remain unbridged/immaterial pending cash-flow classification evidence.

### 2.2 Trade receivables and ECL

Balance-sheet trade and other receivables are R497m. Financial trade/other receivables are gross R395m − allowance R5m = net R390m (p.22). The implied nonfinancial residual is R497m − R390m = R107m (prepayments/VAT/deposits), not separately quantified in this capture. Trade receivables table: gross R374m, allowance R5m, net R369m; other financial receivables are therefore about R21m net. The ECL matrix shows 0% current/30-day/61–90-day expected loss rates and 100% on >90-day; zero rates alone prove neither adequacy nor manipulation.

The extract establishes standard 30-day trade terms and maturity-based ECL, but **does not establish simplified lifetime-ECL election or significant-financing-component policy**. No Stage 1/2 SICR allegation is made. Policy/financing component and portfolio-specific support for zero rates remain NOT VERIFIED.

### 2.3 Tax, NAV and shares

PBT is R(92)m; the tax line is a R29m credit, so signed tax expense is R(29)m. Conventional signed ETR = (−29)/(−92) = **31.5%**. If stated as tax credit/pre-tax loss, the sign is −31.5%. DTA R173m and DT liabilities R513m are not supported by a captured recoverability note.

Closing ordinary shares: 784,313,242 issued less 3,662,209 treasury = **780,651,033**. NAV = parent ordinary equity R1,329m / 780.651m = **R1.70/share**. Share capital R5,410m is not NAV. Weighted-average EPS shares are 782.1m; basic LPS −63/782.1 = **−8.06c**, consistent with (8.1)c.

### 2.4 Historical-book ECL rollforward

Read dated movements as six-month intervals:
- 30 Sep 2024–31 Mar 2025: total impairment loss R173m; acquired loss R96m; historical loss R77m.
- 31 Mar 2025–30 Sep 2025: total impairment loss R26m; acquired gain R156m; historical loss R182m.
- FY25 historical loss = R77m + R182m = **R259m**; FY25 total impairment = R173m + R26m = R199m, agreeing to the annual note.
- 30 Sep 2025–31 Mar 2026: total impairment loss R167m; acquired gain R24m; historical loss R191m.

Do not annualise the H1 loss or treat it as a full-year result. Current H1 historical-book loss R191m exceeds prior H1 R77m and prior H2 R182m, but vintage and FX limitations are not disclosed; that trend cannot be fully attributed without evidence.

### 2.5 Cash flow, debt/overdraft/lease bridge

Statutory cash flow reconciles: operating R(111)m + investing R(61)m + financing R178m = net R6m; opening R107m + net R6m + FX nil = closing R113m. Balance-sheet cash R112m plus R1m held-for-sale cash = R113m.

Debt bridge: interest-bearing debt increased R300m (R3,743m − R3,443m) versus cash net proceeds/settlements of R303m (R1,145m − R842m), leaving **R3m unexplained**. Overdrafts fell R91m and net draw/settlement was R(91)m, reconciling. Lease liabilities fell R19m versus R30m repayment, leaving **R11m unexplained** non-cash/lease movement. These residuals are not plugged; they are small but NOT fully verified.

### 2.6 Liquidity and going concern

Only **R475m undrawn overdraft facilities** is quantified (p.14). Management’s ~R0.8bn “unutilised facilities” lacks a full facility-by-facility schedule. Interest-bearing liabilities include senior R3,547m, subordinated R196m, overdrafts R273m; R1,396m matures within 12 months. Pledged security includes PBD R3,845m and other assets (pp.13–15). The facility-renewal evidence from p.35 is historical/qualitative and tied to the Mobalyz settlement; it is not a current dated funding forecast. No static funding shortfall is asserted. The directors’ going-concern statement (p.36) is management evidence, not assurance; the half-year statements are unreviewed by the auditors (pp.6–7).

### 2.7 Governance

Board changes effective 1 Jan 2026 are historical facts: Jawno became Executive Chairman to oversee transition; Kana remained lead independent; Naidoo joined the board and committees; Amoils/Moggee became joint CEOs. Committee composition, independence classification and transition mandate after 1 Jan 2026 are not in the capture; governance conclusion remains open but is not a primary financial-model input.

## 3. Audit of Common Surface-Reading Pitfalls

1. **Treating R431m POCI cumulative impairment gain as loss coverage is wrong.** Gross R3,414m + gain R431m = net R3,845m. The sign is favourable relative to acquisition base-case, not a conventional reserve.
2. **Applying one ECL regime to both portfolios is wrong.** POCI principal book is amortised cost with lifetime ECL changes; other purchased book debt is FVTPL and reconciled by cash collections and fair-value movements.
3. **Calling zero trade-receivable matrix rates proof of manipulation is unsupported.** Simplified lifetime-ECL policy is not established in the capture; zero rates require support but not a Stage 1/2 migration allegation.
4. **Comparing net PBD collections directly to carrying-value change without income/acquisition adjustments is wrong.** The component bridge uses additions, CAEIR, impairment, FV and collections; a small R6m cash-flow classification residual remains.
5. **Treating 12-month maturities minus cash/undrawn overdraft as a forecast shortfall is wrong.** No dated cash flow, conditions, covenant ratios or facility-by-facility maturities are provided.
6. **Using share capital as NAV is wrong.** NAV is R1,329m / 780.651m = R1.70/share.
7. **Summing one six-month ECL movement as full-year is wrong.** FY25 historical ECL loss is R77m + R182m = R259m, not one half.

## Stage 2 handoff: unresolved material dependencies

**Stage 2 valuation must be withheld while material dependencies remain unresolved.** Do not declare a 99% confidence baseline or publish a target/recommendation.

1. **Liquidity/facility/covenant headroom — material OPEN.** Evidence: pp.5,8,12,14–15,35–36. Only R475m undrawn overdraft is quantified; management’s ~R0.8bn is unscoped. 12-month maturities R1,396m. No facility-by-facility maturities, covenant ratios, conditions, restricted/pledged cash, or dated cash forecast. Needed: full facility schedule, covenant compliance, renewal/conditions, security, forecast collections and portfolio purchases.
2. **POCI lifetime/tail methodology — material NOT VERIFIED.** Evidence: pp.13,21,24. Arithmetic reconciles, but rolling 12-month cash-flow modelling and lifetime/tail methodology for R3,845m POCI are not disclosed. Needed: methodological support, collection assumptions and sensitivity.
3. **PBD cash-flow classification — material classification NOT VERIFIED.** Evidence: pp.12–13,29,31. Other FVTPL reconciles; total PBD carrying decrease R188m versus statutory PBD operating asset movement R182m leaves R6m unidentified. Needed: cash-flow statement mapping of collections, acquisition spend, CAEIR/impairment and collection costs.
4. **Trade-receivable simplified ECL policy and nonfinancial bridge — NOT VERIFIED.** Evidence: pp.20,22,25. Financial net R390m; nonfinancial residual R107m; trade ECL gross R374m, allowance R5m. Policy/financing component not established; zero matrix rates require portfolio-specific support. Needed: policy confirmation, aging/loss experience, forward-looking support.
5. **Tax/DTA recoverability — support missing.** Evidence: pp.8–9. ETR computes to 31.5% on signed basis, but DTA R173m/DTL R513m note is not captured. Needed: DTA recoverability assumptions, temporary differences, tax rate reconciliation.
6. **Historical-book vintage/FX attribution — partial.** Evidence: pp.24,35. Dated ECL arithmetic available: FY25 historical loss R259m; H1 FY26 historical loss R191m. Vintage and FX breakdown absent. Needed before attributing trend to credit deterioration versus mix/FX.
7. **Governance committee independence — non-material financial model gap.** Evidence: pp.7,37. Post-1 Jan 2026 committee composition/independence not captured. Not a primary valuation blocker.

**Consequence:** Stage 2 may use verified historical arithmetic, but cannot publish valuation, target, rating, upside or capital-return recommendation until the material liquidity, POCI methodology, PBD cash-flow classification/TRE ECL support and tax dependencies are resolved.
