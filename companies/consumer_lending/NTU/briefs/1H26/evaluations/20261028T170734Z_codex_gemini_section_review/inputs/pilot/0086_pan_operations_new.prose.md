## 2. Footnotes, qualifications and reconciliations

- **Capture quality:** the p.48–49 tables are heavily column-shifted in extraction; several figures are run together (e.g. "13,807,025 11,566,768", "1,271 855"). Treat individual cell-to-column mapping as provisional; totals are more reliable than segment attributions. No footnotes/definitions for cost metrics (cash cost, AISC, AIC, adjusted EBITDA) are in this capture — they sit in D008 (APMs, pp.38–47) and D010 (Glossary, pp.51–52).
- **Production arithmetic check:** underground 105,478 + surface 2,428 + tailings 113,613 + third-party 18,667 + surface feedstock 32,124 = 272,310 oz, matching the stated Group total (p.49). FY25: 93,724 + 2,654 + 98,636 + 1,081 + 1,513 = 197,608 vs stated 196,527 — a ~1,081 oz gap, consistent with the FY25 third-party buy-in line possibly being double-counted or a rounding/attribution difference. **Unresolved; flagged below.**
- **Buy-in feedstock surge:** third-party buy-in tonnes rose from 92,536 t to 887,646 t and produced oz from 1,081 to 18,667. This materially changes Group head grade (0.5 g/t) and cost metrics; the "excluding third-party buy-in feedstock" AISC line (1,798 vs 1,867 US$/oz) isolates this. Scope must be matched before comparing Group AISC year-on-year.
- **Tennant ramp-up:** surface feedstock tonnes 85,316 → 751,914 t; oz 1,513 → 32,124; A$ revenue 5.9m → 195.3m; A$ AIC 58.1m → 209.3m. A$ AIC/oz FY25 (58.1m/1,513 oz ≈ 38,400 A$/oz) is distorted by ramp-up; FY26 (209.3m/32,124 ≈ 6,516 A$/oz) more representative. FY25 A$ AISC 80,962 A$/kg and AIC 1,584,835 A$/kg appear anomalous — likely low-volume distortion.
- **Cost direction:** Group US$ cash cost +12%, AISC +17%, AIC +8% YoY despite higher gold price; ZAR cash cost/tonne rose 216 → 228 ZAR/t. Barberton AISC rose sharply (2,065 → 2,589 US$/oz); Evander AISC fell (2,090 → 1,914).
- **Revenue vs cost of production (ZAR):** 17,249.1 − 6,431.7 = 10,817.4 ZARm gross margin (FY25: 9,715.6 − 5,084.0 = 4,631.6). Adjusted EBITDA 663.1 US$m × 16.90 ≈ 11,206 ZARm — broadly consistent with margin plus other items; not a full reconciliation.
- **Currency:** Group revenue reported in ZAR but EBITDA in US$; A$ operations (Tennant) reported separately. Do not sum ZAR and A$ revenue lines.
