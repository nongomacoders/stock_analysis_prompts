## Section D002.4 — Changes to the board of directors (p. 7)

### 1. Key facts and figures
- **Other information (p. 7):** the condensed consolidated financial statements, including forward-looking financial information, have **not been reviewed or reported on by Nutun's external auditors**; responsibility rests with the directors. This is a material qualification on all figures in the report.
- **Board changes, all effective 1 January 2026 (p. 7):**
  - Ian Kirk resigned as Chairman, and from the Remuneration and Nominations Committee and Social, Ethics and Sustainability Committee.
  - Jonathan Jawno appointed **Executive Chairman** (to oversee business transition) and member of the Social, Ethics and Sustainability Committee. Suresh Kana remains **lead independent director**.
  - Albertinah Kekana resigned from the Board and from the Audit and Risk, Remuneration and Nominations, and Social, Ethics and Sustainability Committees.
  - Megandra Naidoo (ex-CFO, Life Healthcare) appointed to the Board and to the Audit and Risk, Remuneration and Nominations, and Social, Ethics and Sustainability Committees.
  - Robert Amoils (CEO Nutun South Africa) and Ruben Moggee (CEO Nutun International) appointed to the Board as **joint CEOs**.
- No figures, periods, currencies or units in this section; it is governance disclosure only.
