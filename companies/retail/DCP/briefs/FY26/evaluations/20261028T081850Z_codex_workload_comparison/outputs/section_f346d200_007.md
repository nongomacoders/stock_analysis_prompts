### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **No footnote markers (`[^1]`, `*`) are present in this capture.** The pages are continuous policy text with no table footnotes. No missing-footnote verification is required for this section.

- **Advertising-agreement judgement (note 3.6)** `[D003, p. 20]`: This is the most forecast-relevant qualification. Reimbursements from suppliers are split: (a) recognised as **other income** only where the manufacturer receives an identifiable, separable advertising service and the Group can reasonably estimate fair value; (b) any **excess over estimated fair value is a reduction of cost of sales**. Implication: gross margin and other income are both sensitive to management's fair-value estimate of advertising services — a reclassification between the two lines is possible without changing operating profit. Must be verified against Note 3.6/8/16 amounts (Q-9348bd1461904198, Q-a99837e8ec864376).

- **Dis-Chem Oncology control judgement (note 3.6)** `[D003, p. 20]`: 50% shareholding but consolidated because the Group appoints the majority of directors and controls operations. Implication: 100% of Oncology revenue/EBITDA is consolidated with a 50% NCI attribution; forecasters must not treat it as an equity-accounted JV. Verify Oncology contribution and NCI share in segment/NCI notes (D005).

- **Deferred tax assets on assessed losses (note 3.6)** `[D003, p. 20]`: Recognition depends on forecast taxable profits aligned to executive-approved budgets (growth rates, margins, expenditure). Implication: a forecast downgrade could trigger DTA derecognition and a tax-charge spike. Verify DTA balance and unrecognised assessed losses in the tax note (D005, Note 27.3).

Implication: goodwill/intangibles carrying values depend on the same budgets underpinning the forecast — check goodwill balance and any FY2026 impairment in D005.
