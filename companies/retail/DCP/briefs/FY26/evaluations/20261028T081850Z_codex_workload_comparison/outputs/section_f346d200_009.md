- **Evidence & Answer**: The Group acquired 100% of I-Dis-Chem (Pty) Ltd on 1 August 2025 for R26.7 million (cash paid R29.1m, net of R5.6m cash acquired). ### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

**Footnote (1) on p. 27 — Trade and other receivables fair value**
- **Quote**: "The carrying amount reflects the gross contractual amounts receivable and we expected to receive the full amount at date of acquisition."
- **Implication**: No ECL allowance was recognised on the R396k receivables acquired with I-Dis-Chem. This is a small balance but signals management's confidence in collectability at acquisition date. Must verify in Note 17 (D005/D006) whether any post-acquisition impairment has been recognised on these receivables.

**Footnote (1) on p. 29 — Material items threshold**
- **Quote**: "Items are only disclosed if material in that segment, based on 10% of consolidated profit before tax."
- **Implication**: The segmental material items table is **incomplete by design** — only items exceeding 10% of consolidated PBT (R139.5m) are shown. This means smaller but potentially important cost lines (e.g., IT, professional fees, travel) are omitted. The R10,093.5m "Other expenses (excluding depreciation and amortisation)" line in the segment table is therefore not fully decomposed. Must verify in Note 8 (D005/D006) whether the full other expenses breakdown reconciles to this R10,093.5m figure.

**Footnote (1) on p. 32 — Lease payments**
- **Quote**: "Lease payments relate to variable lease payments of R4.7 million (2025: R2.6 million) in the Group and R0.8 million (2025: R0.7 million) in the company not included in the measurement of lease liabilities (for example, turnover based rental) and R163 million (2025: R86 million) in Group and R65.6 million (2025: R57.1 million) in company relating to short-term leases. There was no expense in the current or prior period relating to low-value assets."
- **Implication**: Short-term lease costs nearly doubled (R86m → R163m), suggesting either more short-term lease arrangements or a change in lease terms. Variable lease payments also increased (R2.6m → R4.7m), potentially indicating turnover-linked rental agreements. These are **not** included in IFRS 16 lease liabilities and therefore represent off-balance-sheet occupancy costs. Must verify in Note 22 (D005/D006) the total lease liability, ROU depreciation, and lease finance charges to reconcile the full occupancy cost picture.

**Loyalty programme note (p. 31)**
- **Quote**: "The point system came to an end in the current financial period and was replaced with direct discounts at point of sale."
- **Implication**: The contract liability for loyalty points (Note 26) should have been released to revenue in FY2026. This explains the 60.6% fall in deferred revenue from R86,912k to R34,242k (Q-b8bbff1173f34f0d). Must verify in Note 26 (D005/D006) that the release is fully accounted for and that no residual liability remains.

**Revenue recognition policy (p. 31)**
- **Quote**: "No element of financing is deemed present as the sales are made with a credit term of 30 to 60 days, which is consistent with market practice."
- **Implication**: Wholesale revenue is recognised at a point in time with no financing component. Retail revenue is recognised at point of sale. The policy does not explicitly address **customer financial services revenue** (finance charges, initiation fees, monthly service fees, credit life insurance) — this is a critical gap. Must verify in Note 7 (D005/D006) whether financial services revenue is included in the R42,825.8m revenue figure or disclosed separately.

**Finance costs policy (p. 33)**
- **Quote**: "All borrowing costs are recognised in profit or loss using the effective interest method as there are no qualifying assets."
- **Implication**: No borrowing costs were capitalised to inventory or PPE. This is consistent with a retail business but means all finance costs (R554.1m) flow through P&L. The lease liability finance charge of R252.4m is the largest component, followed by bank overdraft R145.7m and bank loans R155.8m. Must verify in Note 22/23 (D005/D006) the split between lease and loan interest to reconcile to cash finance costs paid (Q-8113f4ff41654e36).
