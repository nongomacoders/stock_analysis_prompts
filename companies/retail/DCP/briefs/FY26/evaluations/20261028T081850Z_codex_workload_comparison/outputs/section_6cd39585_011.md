- **No footnote markers or footnote definitions are present on either captured page.** Both pages are chart/table slides with bullet commentary; there is no `[^n]`, `*`, or note block. No missing-footnote question is raised for these pages.
- **CID-encoded glyphs**: nearly all numeric and text content on pp. 15–16 is rendered as `(cid:xxxx)` glyphs. Decoded values are reconstructed from the CID mapping and must be treated as unverified until a plain-text re-capture. This is an encoding artefact, not a report qualification.
- **Period qualification (p. 15)**: the sales table compares two non-annual windows — "01 March 2025 to 20 October 2025" vs "21 October 2025 to 19 May 2026". These are ~34-week and ~30-week periods respectively, not equal-length periods and not full financial years. Any growth read-across to FY2026 revenue is therefore invalid without normalisation. This is the single most consequential qualification in the capture.
- **"Since launch" basis (p. 16)**: the R1,126.9m total discount value and its boost-type split are cumulative since programme launch, not FY2026-only. They cannot be used as an annual P&L cost without a launch date and a period split.
- **"Total Income Margin" (p. 15)**: 30.8% vs 31.6% is presented as a Better Rewards cohort metric; it is not reconciled to the group total-income margin of 30.9% cited on p. 6. Scope (cohort vs group) is unconfirmed.
- **"Volume Share Gain" (p. 26 chart)**: the chart shows share gain by competitor/retailer category with no stated base, period, or source. It is a directional claim, not a reconcilable metric.
