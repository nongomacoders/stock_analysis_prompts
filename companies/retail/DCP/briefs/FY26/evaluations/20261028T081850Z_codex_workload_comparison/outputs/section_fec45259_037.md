### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **`[^1]` "Other consists of common control reserve, share-based payments, shares repurchased and foreign currency translation reserve."** `[D003, physical PDF p. 110]` — The "Other reserves" column of R(4,566,716)k is a composite of four distinct reserves; the statement of changes in equity does not split them. This matters because the R35,337k share-based payment expense and R(31,347)k exercise movement net to near-zero within "Other", masking the true cash/equity cost of the scheme. Verify the reserve-by-reserve breakdown in D006.
- **Statement of changes in equity — "Profit/total comprehensive income for the year" subtotal R1,036,581k vs "Profit for the year, net of taxation" R1,035,618k** `[D003, physical PDF p. 110]` — the R963k difference is OCI. Note FY2026 profit attributable to equity holders fell to R978,894k from R1,179,809k (−17.0%) despite revenue +9.3% — the core earnings deterioration driver must be traced (margin compression + higher D&A + higher finance costs).
- **Dividends paid R524,221k (FY2025 R471,989k)** vs **DPS interim 29.4c + final declared 15.9c = 45.3c (FY2025 27.0c + 27.9c = 54.9c)** `[D003, physical PDF p. 110]` — total DPS fell 17.5% while cash dividends paid rose 11.1%; the cash figure includes NCI dividends R30,403k and prior-year final. Implied payout on HEPS 113.7c = 39.8% (FY2025 54.9/137.5 = 39.9%) — consistent ~40% target, but confirm against stated policy in D002.
- **EBITDA footnote `*`** `[D003, physical PDF p. 113–114]`: "EBITDA – operating profit less depreciation and amortisation plus share of profit from associates and joint ventures." This definition **adds** equity-accounted earnings into EBITDA (R48,179k FY2026; R20,207k FY2025) — a non-standard inclusion that flatters EBITDA and the 7.4% margin. Adjust for forecasting.
- **Segmental "Total income margin"** uses total income (gross profit + other income) ÷ external revenue, not gross margin. Retail 31.1% vs wholesale 7.2% — the wholesale margin is on total wholesale revenue including intergroup, so it is not comparable to external-only economics.
- **No footnote text is missing** from this capture; all visible markers (`[^1]`, `*`) have definitions present.
