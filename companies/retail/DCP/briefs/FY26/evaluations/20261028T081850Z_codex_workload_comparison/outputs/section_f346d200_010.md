### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **`*` footnote on the tax rate reconciliation** `[D003, physical PDF p. 35]`: "Other mainly includes **assessed losses not raised**, tax rate differences, sale of property, plant and equipment, dividends withholding tax and leave pay provision." This is the only place in the capture where unrecognised assessed losses are acknowledged. It means the 25.75% ETR is flattered/affected by a 0.32% "Other" bucket that mixes recurring items (leave pay, DWT) with non-recurring ones (assessed losses not raised, PPE disposals). **Forecast implication**: the FY2027 ETR should not be extrapolated mechanically from 25.75% — the recurring component needs isolating in the DTA note (D005). Also note the "Investment in associates and joint ventures" line of **(0.93)%** (2025: (0.33)%) — a non-deductible/equity-accounted tax drag that has nearly tripled; verify whether this is a permanent structural drag or a one-off (e.g. the JV intangible impairment of R18,324k being non-deductible).

- **IFRIC 23 qualification** `[D003, physical PDF p. 35]`: Management "assessed and concluded that the interpretation did not have an impact on its financial statements for the years presented" because "the Group does not operate in a complex multinational environment." This is a clean conclusion — no uncertain tax provision is carried. **Verification**: confirm in D005 that no uncertain-tax provision balance exists and that the cross-border operations (Namibia/Botswana/Eswatini per the subsidiary names in stored questions) are genuinely not "complex multinational."

- **Note 11 HEPS reconciliation — the R18,324k JV intangible impairment** `[D003, physical PDF p. 36]`: This is added back to arrive at headline earnings, so it is excluded from HEPS but **included in basic EPS**. It is the single largest reconciling item between EPS (114.2c) and HEPS (113.7c) and is non-recurring in nature (nil prior year). **Forecast implication**: FY2027 HEPS base should exclude it; but the impairment signals stress at a JV (likely Dis-Chem Life or Kaelo per stored questions) that needs identification in D005 Note 4/5.

- **Note 11 share roll-forward — treasury activity** `[D003, physical PDF p. 36]`: Treasury shares acquired (569,379) vs exercised (606,869) — net treasury release of 37,490 shares, a trivial net buyback in FY2026 versus FY2025's near-neutral (2,390,752 acquired vs 2,397,586 exercised). **Forecast implication**: no meaningful buyback capital allocation in FY2026; the 860,084,483 issued count is static, so DPS × 857.0m weighted shares is the correct dividend base.

- **Note 12 ROU depreciation policy** `[D003, physical PDF p. 37]`: ROU depreciated over the **shorter of lease term or useful life**, with land & buildings ROU over "Lease term." Combined with the stored lease liability of R2,943,233k, this confirms the IFRS 16 depreciation profile is lease-term-driven, not building-life-driven — relevant to the D005 Note 22 reconciliation of ROU depreciation vs lease finance charges.

- **Note 12 useful-life ranges** `[D003, physical PDF p. 37]`: Leasehold improvements 4–12 years, furniture & fixtures 2–12 years, computer hardware 1–9 years. The wide ranges imply judgement; no residual value or useful-life change disclosure in this capture — verify in D005 PPE note whether any useful-life/residual revisions occurred in FY2026.
