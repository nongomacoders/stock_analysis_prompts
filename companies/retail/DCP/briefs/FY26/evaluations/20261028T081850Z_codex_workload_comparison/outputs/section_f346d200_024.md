### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **No footnote markers** (`[^1]`, `*`, etc.) appear in the supplied pages 82–85. No missing-footnote issue arises here.
- **Covenant wording anomaly** `[D005, p. 82]`: "does not expect these ratios to be met in the next 12 months" — as printed, this reads as a covenant breach expectation, contradicting "sufficient head room". Likely a drafting error ("breached"), but must be verified against the covenant note in D006. Material for going-concern and forecast.
- **"These facilities have not been used at year end"** `[D005, p. 82]`: refers to the ABSA/Standard Bank/Nedbank guarantees and the R400m CJ Pharmaceutical guarantee. Yet the maturity table shows bank overdraft R1,666,720k drawn — so the "unused" statement must refer to the guarantee/security lines, not the working capital facilities. Needs reconciliation.
- **Gearing 2.3 (2025: 2.5)** `[D005, p. 82]`: "excluding finance leases" — definition of numerator/denominator not stated; cannot be reconciled to reported net debt without the covenant note.
- **Interest rate sensitivity R46m (2025: R33m)** `[D005, p. 82]`: based on average balance of funds during the period, not year-end balance — so it understates/overstates year-end exposure depending on intra-year debt path. Note the 39% YoY increase in sensitivity vs the modest increase in facilities.
- **Note 31 S189 restructuring** `[D005, p. 83]`: no rand cost, no headcount reduction number, no timing disclosed. This is a potential FY2027 one-off restructuring charge and/or employee cost saving — must be quantified in D006 (employee costs / other expenses).
- **Note 31 new R500m bullet at 3M JIBAR + 1.3%** `[D005, p. 83]`: post-year-end, so not in FY2026 finance costs. Adds ~R500m × (JIBAR+1.3%) to FY2027 finance costs — needs JIBAR assumption. Also increases net debt and leverage covenant headroom pressure.
- **Note 31 FX/fuel** `[D005, p. 83]`: "not had a material impact … continues to be assessed" — unquantified; relevant to FY2027 gross margin (imported goods, freight).
- **Shareholder analysis** `[D005, pp. 84–85]`: total issued 860,084,483; treasury 3,974,878; net 856,109,605. Beneficial >3% total 69.39%; fund managers >3% total 47.03%.
