# 3. Audit of Common Surface-Reading Pitfalls

1. **Misreading “18 324” as R18.324m JV impairment.** It is two columns: **R18k FY2026** and **R324k FY2025**. Correct HEPS reconciliation only works with R18k/R324k.
2. **Treating p.5 decoded figures as audited.** p.5 “gross profit R1,857m” is actually operating profit; revenue R42,606m vs audited R42,825.8m; DPS 43.0c vs audited 45.3c. Use the AFS primary statements.
3. **Treating “total income R13,216m / 30.9%” as audited.** Audited total income is **R13,206,328k / 30.84%**. The R13,216m/30.9% is a presentation rounding/definition artefact.
4. **Treating p.106 “bank loans (1,745)” as total loans payable.** Audited loans payable are **R2,027,578k**; the R282m gap is unexplained. Use Note 23.
5. **Treating company lease future finance charges R661,624k as lease-only.** It is total future finance charges on all company financial liabilities. Lease-only is **R532,085k**; lease PV is **R2,349,702k**.
6. **Treating statutory OCF R1,792,711k as FCF.** It is after finance costs, tax and dividends. Cash from trading operations is R3,124,134k. FCF requires capex, lease principal, finance costs and working-capital normalisation.
7. **Treating “underlying” +20.1% PBT / +17.7% HEPS as statutory.** These are non-IFRS adjustments for R330m X/bigly labs, R115m non-recurring and prior-year property gain. They are not separately presented in the AFS and cannot be reconciled as supplied.
8. **Treating “satisfactory-paid %” or “collection rate” as disclosed.** They are not. Only ECL rates by age bucket are disclosed. The 60–90 day bucket grew 160% while its ECL rate fell to 1.7%; flat 1.18% coverage is not proof of adequacy.
9. **Treating flat ECL coverage 1.18% as adequate.** Gross trade receivables grew 18.4%; other receivables/accrued income ~R1.72bn is unaged; Company >120 day ECL is 92.9% but Group >120 day is 18.7%. The mix matters.
10. **Treating store count 358 as reconciled to trading space.** The p.25 retail-space table is CID-encoded and footnote [^45] is missing. No sales per m²/trading density is disclosed. The p.4 “163,000m²” is unverified.
11. **Treating the p.21 wholesale revenue table as reliable.** It is internally inconsistent: R316m + R280m + R1,329m = R1,925m, not R2,911m; prior sum R1,747m, not R2,787m; printed 16.0% growth vs 4.45% from totals. Use the AFS segmental note: external wholesale R6,233,445k; intergroup R27,810,864k; total R34,044,309k.
12. **Treating Note 31 as containing the final dividend.** It does not. The 15.92c final dividend is in the directors’ report and Note 21. Note 31 covers FX/fuel, the R500m bullet and S189 only.
12. **Treating “facilities have not been used at year end” as no overdraft.** The overdraft is R1,666,720k drawn. The statement refers to guarantee/security lines, but this is not reconciled.
13. **Treating “does not expect these ratios to be met” literally.** It is likely a typo for “breached”. No covenant thresholds or headroom are disclosed; the wording anomaly is unresolved.
14. **Treating gearing 2.6x as net debt/equity or net debt/EBITDA.** The definition is not disclosed. Net debt/equity is ~0.56x; net debt/EBITDA ~1.0x; total liabilities/equity ~2.64x. The 2.6x cannot be reconciled without the definition.
15. **Treating R965m SCF payables as ordinary trade payables without covenant risk.** They sit in trade payables; if reclassified to borrowings, net debt and leverage covenants would deteriorate.
12. **Treating R965m SCF payables and R239m SCF loan as the same item.** Note 23’s R239m Supply Chain Finance is an import SCF loan at SONIA-like rates; Note 24’s R965m is supplier-finance payables in trade payables. They are distinct.
13. **Treating the R500m bullet as FY2026 debt.** It is post-year-end (Note 31). It affects FY2027 finance costs and net debt, not FY2026.
14. **Treating the R103m property gain as FY2026.** It is FY2025 other income (Note 8: R103,692k IFRS 16 release). It explains the difference between AFS operating profit −11.9% and CFO presentation −7.4%.
12. **Treating “R1.1269bn Better Rewards discount value” as FY2026 cost.** It is cumulative since launch, not annual. The 17-week cohort straddles year-end. No annual cost or liability is disclosed.
13. **Treating “R330m X/bigly labs” as capex or opex.** The AFS does not separately disclose it. It could be opex, capex or investment; the split is not given.
14. **Treating “R470m bonus pool” as budget.** It reconciles to Note 8 bonuses R469,971k, so it is the FY2026 bonus expense, not a budget.
15. **Treating “R202m unrecognised assessed losses” as a recognised DTA.** It is unrecognised. DTA on tax losses is only R69,290k.
16. **Treating “goodwill no impairment” as no impairment risk.** 63.2% of goodwill is in Baby/Pure Pharmacy; no CGU headroom sensitivity is disclosed; the wholesale discount rate fell from 15.7% to 14.02%.
17. **Treating “R189m preference shares” as the original Dis-Chem Life 50% acquisition.** The original 50% cost R155.9m (1 Aug 2024); the R189m preference share investment is FY2026. Company carrying value R344,940k vs Group equity-accounted R265,323k.

19. **Treating “R230k non-audit fees” as the only non-audit figure.** Note 8 says R315k. The R85k difference is unresolved.
