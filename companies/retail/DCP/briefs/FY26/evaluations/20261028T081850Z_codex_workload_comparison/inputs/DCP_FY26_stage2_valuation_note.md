1. **Ongoing Ecosystem OPEX:** Unlike the R115m one-off loyalty transition, the R330m ecosystem spend includes recurring cloud, data, and software infrastructure costs that will continue into FY27.

2. **Total income margin falls below 30.5%:** Signal that competitive pharmacy pricing pressure is eroding supplier rebate capacity.
3. **Like-for-like sales growth slows below 3.5%:** Would indicate that consumer traffic gains from Better Rewards are waning.
4. **Net debt (excluding leases) rises above R3.75bn:** Would exhaust remaining banking facility headroom and heighten refinancing costs.
