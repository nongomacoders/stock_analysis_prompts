### 7. Updated Recommendation & Conviction

**Recommendation: SELL (Reaffirmed, Target Lowered to R66.00, -28.6%).**

The disclosure evidence does not overturn a single pillar of the Stage 2 thesis — it reinforces all of them. The specific ECL charge of R273.1m validates the debtor-book distress; the R2.0bn FCFE outflow validates the structural cash-burn call; the "dividends remain suspended" language validates the capital-preservation mandate; and the SAP sequencing (credit management only post finance go-live) validates the no-near-term-ECL-relief assumption. The FY26 base year is modestly worse than Stage 2 assumed, which is why the target falls from R71.00 to R66.00 at an unchanged 11.0x multiple.

The reference price of R92.50 sits ~40% above our R66.00 target — squarely in **Strong Sell** territory on the re-anchored dynamic bands. There is no upgrade catalyst within the 12-month horizon; the only path to a re-rating is a sustained FCFE inflection, which is not credible before FY28E at the earliest.

**Key risks to the SELL call (upside):** faster-than-expected KZN stabilisation (3 profitable months already); a decisive working-capital release reversing the payables drawdown; a strategic buyer or capital raise at a premium; SAP finance go-live unlocking faster ECL relief than modelled. None are evidenced in this disclosure.
