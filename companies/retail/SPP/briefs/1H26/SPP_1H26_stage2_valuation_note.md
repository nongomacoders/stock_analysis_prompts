**Executive Investment Thesis:** SPP is a tale of two businesses. The Ireland (BWG) engine is intact, compounding operating profit (+3.4% as-adjusted) with a 13.7% GP margin and comfortable €96.7m undrawn headroom. The Southern Africa business has suffered a genuine operational and credit-quality breakdown, swinging from R602.9m PBT to a (R50.9m) loss. The three drivers — KZN DC disruption (R123m), Black Friday misallocation (R212m), and a credit book clean-up (R273m specific provisions) — are partly one-off but the credit provisions represent a run-rate reset. The balance sheet is materially stronger than the P&L suggests (net debt down 24.4% to R7.34bn, gearing 2.73x vs 3.5x covenant), but cash generation has broken down with FCF of (R2.01bn) driven by a R1.84bn payables outflow (~60% timing). The UK disposal removes a loss-making drag but generates no cash. The R2.27bn Giannacopoulos contingent liability is a binary tail risk equal to ~42% of equity. We initiate with a HOLD and R52.00 target, awaiting evidence of sustainable SA margin recovery and positive FCF before turning constructive.

**Structural Headwinds:** (1) SA competitive intensity from discount format expansion; (2) elevated ECL run-rate (R150-200m/half) until overdues stabilise; (3) thin liquidity (net cash & banking facilities R166.5m); (4) dividend suspension likely through FY27E; (5) binary legal tail risk.

**Structural Tailwinds:** (1) Ireland resilience and deleveraging; (2) KZN recovery (3 consecutive profitable months); (3) Feb-Mar GP growth turned positive (+2.3%); (4) cost realignment programme (R33.2m annual savings); (5) portfolio simplification complete post-UK disposal.

4. **Giannacopoulos Adverse Ruling:** Any court ruling against SPP on the R2.1bn claim is catastrophic (~42% of equity) — immediate SELL.
5. **Dividend Reinstatement Delayed Beyond FY27:** If no DPS declared by FY27 interim, income investors exit — de-rating.
