**Executive Investment Thesis.** SPP is a structurally challenged voluntary-trading wholesaler whose H1 FY2026 results reveal a far deeper deterioration than the headline "–56% HEPS" implies. On the as-adjusted 26-week base, continuing operating profit fell –47.2% and PBT –65.1%, with the Southern Africa segment swinging from +R867.7m OP to +R237.7m and posting a PBT loss of –R50.9m. The "recovery" narrative (KZN three consecutive positive months, Feb–Mar GP +2.3%) rests on unaudited, unquantified, and partly pre-ECL datapoints, while the audited numbers show a –R630m SA OP swing, continuing FCF of –R2.0bn, and SA leverage at 3.29x against a 3.50x covenant — only ~0.21x (≈R500m EBITDA) of headroom. The 28 September 2026 SENS confirms FY2026 will underperform FY2025, that ECL provisions "remained elevated" post-period, and that earnings/cash benefits only "build progressively through FY2027." We initiate at SELL with a R38.50 target (–20.3%).

**Structural Headwinds.** (1) Credit is the largest unquantified risk: specific impairment charge R273.1m vs cash-flow provision movement of only R150.7m — a ~R122m unexplained gap, with overdues rising 13.6%→15.9% and no debtors note in the disclosure set. (2) SA covenant headroom is thin and ECL pressure is intensifying post-period. (3) The AWG disposal is net-cash-breakeven — it removes ~£5m of H2 losses but provides zero deleveraging. (4) Capital allocation quality is questionable (Hajimarkos R149.8m goodwill, two stores impaired within two months; GiCom R30.7m write-off; Perrys R79.5m contingent consideration due Feb-2027). (5) Dividend reinstatement is a FY2027+ event at the earliest.

**Structural Tailwinds.** Ireland (BWG) is a genuinely healthy engine — €25.3m OP at 3.0% margin, flat YoY, ~€70m TTM EBITDA, 2.12x gearing, €96.7m facility headroom. SPAR Health grew +26.1% revenue, Build it returned to organic retail growth of +6.1%, and the KZN DC flooring remediation is complete with the overflow facility exited. Cost realignment (VSP R27.3m cost for R33.2m annual savings) and SAP finance go-live are genuine efficiency levers. But these are insufficient to offset the SA credit and margin drag within our forecast horizon.
