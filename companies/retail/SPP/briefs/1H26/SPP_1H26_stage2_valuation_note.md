## 1. EXECUTIVE INVESTMENT THESIS & 12-MONTH JSE PRICE TARGET

**Thesis unchanged post-SENS: a trough-earnings recovery story with a cash-flow asterisk.** The attached interim results package for the 26 weeks ended 27 March 2026 corroborates — and in places sharpens — the forensic decomposition already embedded in our model. Operating profit fell 47.8% to R730.7m (margin 1.1%), driven by the three identified anomalies: KZN (R123m), Black Friday overspend (R212m) and balance-sheet clean-ups (current-period impairments R38m + R28m Build it SIW closure; legacy impairments R143m; credit-risk policy change R73m; credit-risk other R86m). HEPS from continuing operations fell 55.6% to 199.9c. Ireland remains the stabiliser: operating profit +1.8% to €25.3m (R502.8m), GP margin +20bps to 13.7%, ROCE 17.4%, net debt €139.3m (€44m below the 2022 peak), gearing 2.12x with €96.7m unused facility headroom.

**What the SENS adds — and what it does not resolve.** The disclosure set confirms: (i) the UK disposal is contracted (APA signed with A.F. Blakemore, 4 May 2026; Saltash depot and 71 stores; ~£13m gross proceeds, net broadly breakeven; phased completion June–September 2026; 63 further stores in third-party disposal), removing ~£5m of H2 trading losses and the last discontinued-operations drag; (ii) KZN delivered three consecutive months of positive operating profit with OOS materially improved from peak by period-end — asserted, still not independently verifiable; (iii) Feb–Mar GP growth turned positive (+2.3%) on revenue growth of +2.1%, the first genuine margin-recovery evidence; and (iv) the VSP delivers R33.2m annualised savings against R27.3m cost. Against this, the two thesis-critical junctions remain open: H1 FCFE of −R2,011.9m (payables timing flattered the working-capital line by ~R1.7bn) and G&L overdues at 15.9% (from 13.6%) with specific impairment of R273.1m (+143%). New detail: a R168m Giannacopoulos SAP claim (R5.1m provision recognised against a R2.1bn legacy claim that remains unprovided), and prior-period restatements (R457.8m loan classification; R494.0m overdraft reclassification; R672.0m inter-company elimination) that are presentational but require monitoring.

**Why HOLD, not BUY:** the evidence of improvement is real but forward-dated. The investment case now rests on two unproven swing factors — KZN recovery and H2 FCF reversal — and neither will be verifiable until the September 2026 full-year results. Upgrading ahead of that verification would be paying for evidence not yet delivered. Equally, the de-risking (UK exit contracted, portfolio simplification complete, leadership renewed, debt headroom of R5.57bn and R6.64bn total available funding) argues against a SELL. The risk/reward remains asymmetric to the upside only on confirmation, hence HOLD with a R104 target.

**Valuation anchor:** at R92.50 the stock trades ~9.8x FY27E HEPS (945c) and ~5.9x FY27E EV/EBITDA — a distressed multiple discounting much of the bad news. Our R104 target applies 11.0x FY27E HEPS, a ~20% discount to SPP's 5-year average forward P/E (~13.5x), cross-checked by DCF at R106. No change to target or reference price is warranted on this disclosure set: the SENS is confirmatory of the established base case, not incrementally value-adding.

## 6. ACTIONABLE PORTFOLIO MONITORING TRIGGERS & CATALYSTS

**Operational & Financial Red Lines (thesis-breaking — would trigger de-rating to SELL):**
1. **Debtor overdues breach 18.0%** (from 15.9%) or specific impairment charge exceeds R350m annualised — signals accelerating retailer distress beyond current provisioning; fair value cut to ~R80.
2. **FY26 H2 FCFE fails to reverse to positive** (i.e., full-year FCFE worse than −R1.0bn) — confirms payables timing was structural, not timing; liquidity tightens; fair value cut to ~R75.
3. **Gearing (normalised for payables timing) exceeds 3.2x** or covenant headroom (3.5x) narrows below 0.3x — triggers refinancing risk and likely equity raise; fair value cut to ~R70.
4. **KZN returns to operating loss** for two consecutive months — invalidates the central recovery claim; fair value cut to ~R85.
5. **Group gross margin falls below 10.2%** in FY26 H2 — signals Feb–Mar recovery was not sustained; fair value cut to ~R88.

**Quantified Sensitivity Impacts (rule of thumb):**
- 75bps group gross margin swing = ~95c HEPS = ~R10.50/share fair value.
- 10bps debtor cost % swing = ~25c HEPS = ~R2.75/share fair value.
- 50bps SA sales growth swing = ~40c HEPS = ~R4.40/share fair value.
- 25bps SARB rate move = ~R18m net finance cost = ~9c HEPS = ~R1.00/share.
- R100m working capital outflow = ~52c HEPS-equivalent cash drag = ~R5.70/share FCFE impact.
