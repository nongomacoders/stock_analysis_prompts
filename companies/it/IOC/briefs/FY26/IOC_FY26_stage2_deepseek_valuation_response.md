**Executive Investment Thesis:**

We initiate coverage on iOCO Limited (IOC) with a **BUY** recommendation and a 12-month price target of **R4.75**, implying a 21.8% upside from the reference price of R3.90. Our thesis is built on the following pillars:
1.  **A Cleaner, Higher-Quality Business:** The Stage 1 forensic audit confirms that underlying operations are robust despite the "noisy" FY2026 headline. The core business generates sustainable statutory EBITDA of R591.1m and positive free cash flow. The turnaround from a struggling conglomerate to a lean, decentralised IT services group is fundamentally complete.
2.  **Value Creation Engine Restarted:** The Group has transitioned from a deleveraging, defensive posture to an offensive capital allocation strategy. The reinstatement of a disciplined M&A program, targeting founder-led businesses to fill capability gaps, signals a return to the "serial acquirer" model that historically delivered strong shareholder returns. This provides a clear path to EPS accretion.
3.  **Undervalued Relative to Intrinsic Value:** The market is overly focused on the FY2026 working capital cash outflow and the headline ETR benefit, missing the broader picture. At R3.90, the stock trades at an undemanding ~10x our sustainable EV/EBITDA of R606m (on R591m EBITDA) and ~10.5x FY27E HEPS. This is a significant discount to the intrinsic value of its established book of business and future prospects.
4.  **Balance Sheet Fortress:** The forensic audit confirms a robust balance sheet with net bank debt of just R63m and a comfortable liquidity runway of R667m. This provides ample firepower for the M&A strategy and protects the downside.

**Structural Headwinds & Tailwinds:**
- **Headwinds:** The single most significant near-term risk is working capital management. FY2026 saw a R167.5m cash outflow from payables, which management is now explicitly targeting with new compensation incentives. Failure to rectify this cash conversion cycle will constrain the self-funding of growth. Intense competition in commodity IT services and hardware resale also pressures gross margins, as evidenced by the International segment's contraction.
- **Tailwinds:** The Group is a direct beneficiary of the accelerating demand for cloud, cybersecurity, AI, and data analytics in South Africa and key international markets. Its decentralised model, with 12 autonomous business units, is designed to be more agile and customer-focused, which should drive organic growth and market share gains. The R1.1bn assessed tax loss pool provides a unique structural advantage, enhancing the after-tax returns on profitable acquisitions.

### 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts ('What to Watch For That Will Move the Stock')

**Operational & Balance Sheet Red Lines (Triggers for a Downgrade to HOLD/SELL):**
1.  **Working Capital Deterioration:** A return to a net working capital outflow in H1 FY2027 (due Feb 2027) would indicate that the new 45-day cycle incentives are failing. This would constrain M&A and buyback capacity.
2.  **Contract Asset Write-downs:** Watch for any significant impairments to the R141.1m gross contract assets (unbilled WIP). An increase in the R5.05m allowance would indicate project margin slippage or billing issues.
3.  **Segmental Margin Reversal:** A failure of the International segment's gross margin to recover toward 30%, or a further widening of the central reconciliation drag, would challenge the "operational leverage" thesis.
4.  **Billable Utilisation Dip:** While not explicitly disclosed, any commentary suggesting developer/consultant bench strength is increasing will be a negative signal for margins. Anecdotal evidence of utilisation dropping below 70% would be a red flag.

**Quantified Sensitivity Impacts (Rule of Thumb):**
- Every **100 bps expansion in Group EBITDA margin** shifts FY27E HEPS by approximately **6 cents** and our fair value by **~R0.30/share**.
- Every **100 bps reduction in the effective tax rate** shifts FY27E HEPS by roughly **6 cents** and our fair value by **~R0.30/share**.
- A **R100m increase in net debt** (e.g., for a cash-funded acquisition) would reduce our EV/EBITDA-based valuation by **~R0.16/share**.

**Re-Rating Catalysts (Milestones for an Upgrade to a Higher Target Price):**
1.  **Multi-Year Contract Wins & Value:** The R2.1bn in FY26 "new wins" must be substantiated with details on term, contract value, and recurring margin in H1 FY27 results.
2.  **Value-Accretive M&A Execution:** Completion of the discussed third acquisition and demonstrable progress on an M&A pipeline that utilises the R1.1bn tax loss pool.
3.  **Dividend Resumption:** An announcement of a maiden dividend, even a small one, would signal confidence in the sustainability of free cash flow and attract a new class of income-oriented investors.
4.  **Divisional Profitability Disclosure:** Commencement of reporting that clearly shows the profitability of the 12 new divisions, providing granular visibility and enabling a sum-of-the-parts valuation.
