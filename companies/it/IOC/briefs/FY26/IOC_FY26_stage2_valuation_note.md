### 1. Executive Investment Thesis

**The "Turnaround" is Real, but the "Growth" Story is Elusive.**
iOCO has successfully stabilized its balance sheet, reducing net bank debt to a negligible R63m (0.1x EBITDA) and extending maturities. The restructuring from 4 to 12 business units has injected accountability, evidenced by a 21% increase in Adjusted EBITDA to R624m. However, forensic analysis reveals this profitability was driven by cost-cutting and a lower effective tax rate (15.5%), not top-line momentum.

**Quality of Earnings Concerns Persist.**
While headline HEPS rose 42% to 57c, this was flattered by a R245m surge in low-margin hardware resale (+49.8% YoY) which masked a contraction in core services revenue. Furthermore, cash generation deteriorated significantly, with net operating cash flow falling 43.5% to R239m due to a R167m working capital outflow (payables squeeze). The CEO's admission that working capital management "fell short" validates our caution.

**Valuation & Recommendation.**
At R4.22, IOC trades on ~8.3x FY26 HEPS and ~6.5x EV/EBITDA. While the balance sheet is clean, the lack of organic growth and reliance on low-margin resale justify a "HOLD." We initiate with a 12-month target price of **R4.60**, implying 9% upside, based on a blended 6.5x EV/EBITDA and 9x P/E multiple on our FY27 estimates.

### 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

**Operational & Balance Sheet Red Lines (Sell Triggers)**
1.  **Working Capital Reversal:** Failure to release at least R50m from working capital in H1 FY27 (indicates the 45-day cycle target is failing).
2.  **Hardware Margin Collapse:** Gross margin in the IT Services segment dropping below 28% (indicates pricing pressure or bad deal structuring).
3.  **Key Contract Churn:** Loss of any of the top 5 clients (concentration risk remains high).
4.  **Utilization Dip:** Billable consultant utilization dropping below 70% (currently estimated at ~75%).

**Re-Rating Catalysts (Buy Triggers)**
1.  **Dividend Resumption:** A declaration of a dividend (even a small one) would signal confidence in cash flow sustainability.
2.  **Organic Growth:** Two consecutive quarters of >5% organic revenue growth (excluding hardware resale).
3.  **M&A Execution:** Successful integration of the Astraia acquisition and immediate EPS accretion.
4.  **Tax Efficiency:** Utilization of the R1.1bn tax losses to boost cash flow (requires structuring M&A into specific entities).
