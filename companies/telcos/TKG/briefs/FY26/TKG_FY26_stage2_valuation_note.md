**Executive Investment Thesis:**

TKG Resources presents a compelling institutional investment opportunity, transitioning from a legacy fixed-line operator to a modern, data-led digital infrastructure provider. Our BUY recommendation is anchored on three core pillars:

1.  **Fortress Balance Sheet & Capital Allocation Flexibility:** The forensic audit confirms a net cash position of R1.19bn when excluding lease liabilities, a stark contrast to the headline 0.5x net debt/EBITDA ratio. This, combined with R7.7bn in cash and R6.4bn in undrawn facilities, provides immense strategic optionality. The revised dividend policy (40-60% of FCF) signals management's confidence in sustainable cash generation and a commitment to shareholder returns, with the FY26 dividend already hiked by 65.7%.

2.  **Sustainable, Cost-Driven Earnings Growth:** The 21.5% HEPS growth is not a fluke of revenue momentum but a result of deliberate structural cost optimization. The 5.8% EBITDA growth was driven by a 3.7% reduction in employee costs and a 5.9% drop in maintenance, showcasing a business that is becoming leaner and more efficient. This margin expansion (to 28.1%) is the true engine of profitability and is sustainable, unlike one-off gains.

3.  **Data-Led Growth Offsetting Legacy Decline:** The structural shift from voice to data is being managed effectively. Strong growth in mobile data (+10.5%) and fibre revenue (+8.2%) is successfully offsetting the anticipated 21.8% decline in fixed-voice revenue. The 31.1% surge in mobile data subscribers to nearly 20 million demonstrates the success of the value-led strategy, particularly in the pre-paid segment. This provides a clear, multi-year growth runway.

**Structural Headwinds & Tailwinds:**

*   **Tailwinds:** Accelerating data consumption, smartphone adoption, and demand for fibre and enterprise digital solutions. The "OneTelkom" strategy is unlocking synergies and improving customer stickiness. A strong and growing FCF profile supports both reinvestment and enhanced shareholder returns.
*   **Headwinds:** Intensifying competition in the pre-paid mobile and fibre markets, macro-economic pressures on consumer spending, and regulatory risks (e.g., data rollover regulations). The escalating lease liabilities (R7.6bn) and future committed leases (R359m) represent a growing, non-operating cash flow obligation that must be carefully managed.

### 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

**Key Catalysts (Positive):**
*   **FY27 Interim Results (Nov 2026):** Evidence of sustained mobile service revenue growth (>6%) and further EBITDA margin expansion (>28.5%).
*   **Dividend Declaration (May 2027):** A final dividend in line with or exceeding the 50% payout ratio of FCFE, confirming the sustainability of cash generation.
*   **BCX Turnaround:** Signs of revenue stabilization and margin recovery in the BCX segment, particularly in the Converged Communications business.
*   **Spectrum Auction (2027):** Successful acquisition of additional spectrum to support mobile data growth and network capacity.

**Key Risks & Monitoring Triggers (Negative):**
*   **Red Line 1: Mobile Revenue Growth Slows:** A decline in mobile service revenue growth to below 4% for two consecutive quarters would signal a failure of the core growth strategy and pressure the valuation.
*   **Red Line 2: Lease Liability Escalation:** A significant increase in lease remeasurements or new lease commitments (e.g., >R1.5bn annually) that outpaces EBITDA growth, eroding FCFE.
*   **Red Line 3: Dividend Cut:** Any reduction in the dividend payout ratio or a dividend per share below the prior year would be a major negative signal on management's confidence in FCF.
*   **Macro-Economic Pressure:** A sharp deterioration in South African consumer spending or a significant rise in interest rates, impacting ARPU and finance costs.
*   **Regulatory Intervention:** Adverse outcomes from ICASA regarding data rollover regulations or spectrum licensing that increase costs or limit growth.
