**Executive Investment Thesis**
TKG has delivered a genuine operational turnaround in FY2026, characterized by disciplined cost management (EBITDA margin +1.2ppts to 28.1%), strong free cash flow generation (R3.1bn), and a significantly de-risked balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 0.5x). The mobile business continues to gain market share with service revenue growth of 6.8%, and Openserve has returned to growth (+2.3%) after nine years of decline.

However, the **Stage 1 Forensic Audit** reveals that the quality of these earnings is materially overstated. The reported HEPS growth of 21.5% is flattered by a low tax base (cash tax collapsed 92% to R32m) and non-recurring items (R209m factoring gain, R194m asset disposal gain). Furthermore, the "no impairment" outcome for the BCX goodwill (R1.3bn) rests entirely on a 580bps cut in the discount rate (20.8% to 15.0%), which we view as aggressive given BCX's revenue decline of 8.6%.

While the balance sheet is genuinely strong (R13.4bn liquidity buffer), the stock has rallied 58.4% over the past year and now trades at a forward P/E of ~8.5x and EV/EBITDA of ~3.5x, which is fair for a telco with structural legacy headwinds. We initiate with a **HOLD** rating and a 12-month price target of **R62.50**, implying a modest 9.4% upside plus a 4.8% dividend yield.

**Structural Headwinds vs. Tailwinds**
*   **Tailwinds:** Mobile data monetization (traffic +18.5%), Openserve external revenue growth (+10.7%), and deleveraging enabling higher dividend payouts (policy raised to 40-60% of FCF).
*   **Headwinds:** Legacy voice collapse (-21.8%), BCX structural decline, rising lease costs (IFRS 16 liabilities +9.2%), and a normalizing tax rate that will compress future FCF growth.

### 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

**Red Lines (Sell Triggers)**
*   **Dividend Cut:** If FCF falls below R2.0bn, jeopardizing the 40-60% payout policy.
*   **BCX Impairment:** A write-down of the R1.3bn goodwill (triggered by a discount rate increase >100bps).
*   **Spectrum Costs:** A significant cash outlay for the 2027 spectrum auction (>R2bn) without clear ROI.
*   **Regulatory Shock:** Adverse ruling on the SIU investigation or ICASA data rollover regulations.

**Catalysts (Buy Triggers)**
*   **Mobile Growth:** Sustained double-digit mobile data revenue growth (>10%).
*   **Openserve Monetization:** FTTH connectivity rate exceeding 55%.
*   **Cost Savings:** IT cost optimization reaching the 5% of revenue target ahead of FY28.
*   **Special Dividend:** Declaration of a special dividend from the R7.7bn cash pile.

**Reporting Dates**
*   **1H27 Results:** November 2026.
*   **FY27 Results:** June 2027.
*   **AGM:** August 2026.

**Key Monitoring Metrics**
*   **Cash Tax Rate:** Monitor the normalization from the FY26 trough (R32m) to the expected R1.2bn.
*   **Lease Liabilities:** Track the remeasurement impact (CPI) on the R7.5bn liability.
*   **BCX Margin:** Watch for stabilization in the 9.4% EBITDA margin.
*   **Working Capital:** Monitor the R872m double-count error identified in Stage 1.
