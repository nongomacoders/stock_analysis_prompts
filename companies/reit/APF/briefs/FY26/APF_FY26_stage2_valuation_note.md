### Summary of the 8 Subsequent SENS Disclosures & Valuation Impact (August – September 2026)

Following the publication of our Stage 2 Institutional Valuation Report, Accelerate Property Fund Limited (`APF` or `APFE`) released eight SENS announcements between 6 August 2026 and 25 September 2026. Cross-examining these disclosures against our established forensic model reveals a **materially worse operational cash flow reality at KPMG Crescent** and **heightened regulatory/governance risk**, only partially mitigated by the agreed R385.0 million gross (R359.5 million net) disposal of KPMG Crescent to Rand Mutual Assurance (`RMA`).

Consequently, we **reaffirm our contrarian SELL recommendation** and **lower our 12-Month Target Price by -12.5% from R0.48 per share (48.0 ZAR cents) to R0.42 per share (42.0 ZAR cents)**, implying **-30.0% downside** from the R0.60 reference price.

#### Forensic Synthesis of the 8 SENS Announcements:

1. **SENS 8 (25 Sept 2026) — Disposal of KPMG Crescent & Severe Rent Reset Shock (`20260925_sens_8265`):**
* **Transaction Structure:** APF (via wholly owned subsidiary Parktown Crescent Properties) entered into a Category 2 sale of shares agreement with Rand Mutual Assurance Company Limited (`RMA`) to sell 100% of Wanooka Properties (Pty) Ltd—owner of KPMG Crescent and the 85 Empire Road Parktown parkade (20,096 m² GLA)—for a gross consideration of **R385.00 million** in cash against share transfer (compared to the 31 March 2026 Mills Fitchet external valuation of **R393.00 million**, a **-2.04% gross headline discount**).


* **True Net Cash Proceeds (R359.50 Million — an -8.52% Effective Discount):** APF has contractually agreed to spend **R15.00 million** on maintenance and capital improvements on behalf of RMA ("resulting in a net purchase price pre-commission of **R370.00 million**") and pay **R10.50 million** in broker commissions. Net cash proceeds available for debt reduction are therefore exactly **R359.50 million** (representing an **-8.52% net realisation discount** to the R393.00 million carrying value, or R33.50 million value leakage).


* **Catastrophic Rental Reset Revealed (R432.00/m² down to R99.52/m² on 1 Sept 2026):** In our prior report, Downside Trigger A.2 warned that if KPMG Crescent reset below **R210.00/m²** (versus our base-case assumption of R250.00/m²), Group ICR would fall below 1.0x. SENS 8 reveals that following 12 years of 8% compounding escalations (which generated **~R100.8 million** of FY2026 Cash NOI), the gross rental payable by KPMG **reverted to just R99.52/m² on 1 September 2026** (an astonishing **-77.0% collapse** from the prior R432.00/m² gross billing rate!), and that Wanooka and KPMG agreed a **formal lease exit date of 30 April 2027**.


* **Cash Flow vs. SOTP NAV Impact:** At R99.52/m² across 20,096 m², KPMG Crescent’s annualised gross revenue dropped from ~R104.2 million to **R24.00 million** (R2.00 million/month) from 1 September 2026—a **R80.2 million annualised gross revenue cliff** (more than double our previously modelled R33.0m–R40.0m reset!). Over the 7 months from 1 September 2026 to 31 March 2027, this slices an additional **R16.7 million** directly off FY2027E Cash NPI and FFO, dragging **FY2027E ICR down from 1.13x to 0.99x**. Conversely, on a capital basis, locking in **R359.50 million** of net disposal proceeds for a soon-to-be-vacant single-tenant Parktown office building is a major salvage victory compared to our prior ~R265.0 million post-reset economic valuation (+R94.5 million SOTP gross asset cushion), provided unconditional **Competition Authority approval** is obtained.




2. **SENS 2 (7 Aug 2026) — JSE Public Censure & R500,000 Suspended Fine (`20260807_sens_4931`):**
* **Ongoing Governance Breach:** The JSE imposed a **public censure** and a **R500,000 fine (wholly suspended for 3 years)** on APF for breaching **Paragraph 13.40 of the JSE Listings Requirements**. Specifically, despite announcing on 18 December 2023 that the 5-year appointment of Flanagan & Gerard, Moolman Group, and Luvon Investments (collectively the `Asset Manager`) at Fourways Mall was subject to shareholder approval, APF allowed the Asset Manager to commence operations on site on **1 February 2024** without obtaining majority independent shareholder approval and without inserting the mandatory shareholder right to cancel the contract.


* **Month-to-Month Precariousness & Regulatory Ultimatum:** After the suspensive conditions lapsed in November 2024, the Asset Manager remained on site on a **month-to-month basis**. The JSE ruled that this non-compliance remains **ongoing** and instructed APF to **"forthwith comply with the Listings Requirements"**. This elevates the **31 December 2026** Executive Scorecard deadline to obtain shareholder approval for the Flanagan & Gerard / Moolman agreement from an internal KPI into an urgent regulatory compliance mandate; any further misstep triggers the R500,000 fine and potential JSE enforcement action.

## 2. Updated Property Operating Forecast (FY27E & FY28E)

### Revised Portfolio Physical GLA & Disposal Roll-Forward (Incorporating KPMG Crescent)

Integrating SENS 8 (`20260925_sens_8265`) fundamentally reshapes the Commercial Office segment in both FY2027E and FY2028E:

1. **KPMG Crescent Rent Collapse (1 Sept 2026) & Share Transfer Timing (31 March 2027E):**
* KPMG Crescent (20,096 m² GLA) generated **R100.8 million** of Cash NOI in FY2026 (~R8.40 million/month) on an expiring long-term lease that had escalated at 8% p.a. over 12 years.


* For the **first 5 months of FY2027E (1 April 2026 – 31 August 2026)**, APF continued to collect the peak gross rental of ~R432/m² (~R8.68 million/month gross; ~R42.0 million Cash NOI over 5 months).


* On **1 September 2026**, gross rental reverted to **R99.52/m²** (**R2.00 million/month** gross billing across 20,096 m²) ahead of KPMG’s agreed **30 April 2027 lease exit**. After property operating costs and municipal expenses, net monthly Cash NOI from KPMG Crescent drops from ~R8.40 million/month to just **~R1.15 million/month** (~R8.05 million total over the final 7 months of FY2027E)—bringing full-year FY2027E KPMG Crescent Cash NOI to **~R50.05 million** (a **-R50.75 million YoY drop** vs. FY2026A, and **-R16.70 million worse** than our prior Stage 2 forecast).


* Subject to unconditional **Competition Authority approval** (typically 3–5 months for a Category 2 property transaction), we model the transfer of Wanooka shares to RMA occurring at year-end (**31 March 2027E**), removing **20,096 m²** of Commercial GLA and injecting **R359.50 million** of net cash proceeds (R385.0m gross less R15.0m capex obligation and R10.5m broker commission).




2. **FY2028E Commercial Office Footprint & Vacancy Normalization:**
* With KPMG Crescent (20,096 m²) transferring at 31 March 2027E and the Cape Town Foreshore Held-for-Sale assets (Oceana House 7,597 m² and Thomas Pattullo 5,967 m² = 13,564 m²) modelled to transfer in mid-FY2028E for R175.0 million net proceeds, APF’s retained Commercial Office portfolio by 31 March 2028E shrinks to just **3 properties totaling 14,767 m²**: **Citibank Sandton** (12,453 m²), **KPMG Polokwane** (1,484 m²), and **KPMG Secunda** (830 m²).


* Crucially, selling Wanooka to RMA before KPMG vacates on 30 April 2027 **avoids a 20,096 m² vacancy spike** in FY2028E, leaving APF with only the 2,553 m² vacancy at Citibank Sandton (17.3% of the small 14,767 m² retained office residual, or **5.7% consolidated portfolio vacancy** across 165,210 m² total GLA in FY2028E).

## 4. Updated Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) NAV Valuation & Target Price Bridge

### Reconciliation of SOTP Adjustments vs. Forward FFO Cut

Cross-examining the balance sheet against the 8 subsequent SENS announcements produces two opposing forces in our blended valuation model:

1. **Positive SOTP Capital Salvage on KPMG Crescent (+R94.5 million / +R0.046 per share pre-discount):**
* In our prior Stage 2 SOTP table, we valued the Continuing Commercial Office portfolio (R770.6 million book value, comprising KPMG Crescent at R393.0 million and Citibank/KPMG Polokwane/Secunda at R377.6 million) at **R605.0 million** (a -R165.6 million write-down, which implicitly valued KPMG Crescent at **~R265.0 million** on a post-reset basis and the remaining continuing offices at **R340.0 million**).


* Under SENS 8 (`20260925_sens_8265`), APF has contracted to sell KPMG Crescent to an owner-occupier/institutional buyer (`RMA`) for **R385.0 million gross / R359.5 million net cash** (after deducting the R15.0m maintenance/capex obligation and R10.5m broker commission).


* Moving KPMG Crescent out of continuing offices into **Contracted Disposals at R359.5 million net cash** (while valuing the remaining R377.6 million of continuing offices—Citibank Sandton, KPMG Polokwane, and KPMG Secunda—at **R330.0 million** on a 10.75% cap rate) lifts total warranted Commercial Office + KPMG value from R605.0 million to **R689.5 million** (+R84.5 million net uplift).
* However, this capital salvage is partially offset by:
* **Swap Over-Hedging & CPV Competition Risk (-R8.0 million):** Gross debt dropping to R2,205.5 million against R2,500.0 million of swaps creates an immediate 113.4% over-hedge penalty (increasing the derivative liability adjustment from -R12.0m to **-R18.0m**), while Competition Commission approval risk on the RMA transaction remains open.


* **Heightened Refinancing, Governance & ICR Breach Discount (Increased from 25.0% to 30.0%):** Following the **JSE Public Censure (SENS 2)** for ongoing non-compliance with Paragraph 13.40 at Fourways Mall, the **12.17% AGM minority revolt against share issuance authorities (SENS 5)**, and the **collapse in FY2027E ICR to 0.99x** (breaching even management's 1.10x internal scorecard floor due to KPMG's R99.52/m² rent reset), we increase our institutional SOTP Refinancing, Covenant & Governance Discount from **25.0% to 30.0%**. This keeps our **Risk-Adjusted SOTP Valuation (70% weight) at R0.51 per share** (vs. R0.52 previously).






2. **Severe Contraction in Capitalised Forward FFO / DIPS Valuation (30% Weighting Drops from R0.37 to R0.23 per Share):**
* Because KPMG Crescent was APF’s single highest-yielding cash cow (generating R100.8 million Cash NOI in FY2026, or 23.7% of Group Cash NPI, on virtually zero debt), selling it for R359.5 million net cash saves R36.1 million of annual interest in FY2028E but permanently removes its excess cash flow.


* Consequently, stabilised **FY2028E Recurring FFO drops by -37.8% from R71.43 million (3.49 cps) to R44.43 million (2.17 cps)**.
* Capitalising FY2028E recurring FFO of R44.43 million (2.17 cps) at a **9.50% target exit FFO yield** reduces the **Forward Earnings / Yield Valuation (30% weight) from R0.37 per share (R751.9m) to R0.23 per share (R467.7m)**.


3. **Blended 12-Month Target Price Revision:**
* Combining **70% Risk-Adjusted SOTP (R0.51/share)** and **30% Capitalised FFO Yield (R0.23/share)** yields our updated 12-Month Institutional Target Price of **R0.42 per share (42.0 ZAR cents)**—a **-R0.06 (-12.5%) target price downgrade**.
