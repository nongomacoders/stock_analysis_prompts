**Core institutional thesis.** Calgro M3 is a leveraged, cash-absorbing residential developer whose FY2026 headline profitability (PAT R160.2m, HEPS 156.76c) masks a structurally negative operating cash flow (CFO −R14.8m vs +R107.9m prior year) and a R439.7m net build in construction-contract assets. The reported 27.20% gross margin is a legacy-infrastructure artefact (management's own words: "aided by past infrastructure costs"), not a sustainable run-rate; Residential segment margin compressed 341 bps to 24.24% while Memorial Parks expanded to 54.90%. Roughly 60% of "other income" (R11.5m of R19.1m) is non-cash fair-value gains, and the R7.7m ECL "credit" is a release against a growing receivable book — a quality-of-earnings red flag. The single largest earnings driver of the year was not operations but the R102.1m of borrowing costs capitalised into construction contracts (up 60% YoY), which suppressed P&L finance costs by 28% even as gross debt rose 24.8%.

**Structural headwinds.** (1) **Refinancing cliff:** ~R173m of DMTN notes mature in FY2027 (CGR 52/53/55/56) plus NHFC instalments from May 2026; undiscounted <1yr borrowings R224.3m vs cash R183.7m. (2) **Single-point-of-failure funding:** parent's going concern explicitly dependent on a "commitment" from Calgro M3 Developments; the R1.338bn treasury loan to that subsidiary is the only asset backing R1.330bn parent borrowings; DMTN guaranteed by the same subsidiary. (3) **BDC reclassification distortion:** JV→joint operation effective 1 Sep 2025 removed R165.0m JV loan, added R206m asset, eliminated R11.6m JV profit, debited R19.25m to retained income; FY2027 carries a full year vs 4 months. (4) **R852.1m "future contract asset" bucket** carries no revenue and no ECL — hidden impairment exposure if developments are abandoned pre-contract. (5) **74% of contract assets (R938.1m) in "specific exposure" bucket**, up from R393.9m — a R544.2m reclassification not reflected in the headline ECL charge. (6) **Maintenance provision R66.3m materially unfunded** — ring-fenced fund only R20.7m, leaving ~R45.6m gap.

**Tailwinds.** Memorial Parks cash collections +11.9% to R106.9m, fully covering Group overheads; layby book +29.7% to R59.7m; Residential pipeline >30,000 opportunities worth R29.2bn; BDC infrastructure commenced (Phase 1 bulk/link due end-FY27); non-core project trade-out progressing (70% of units transferred); B-BBEE Level 1 retained; NAV/share +10.8% to R16.47.

## 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

### 6.1 Operational & Balance Sheet Red Lines (3–5 specific triggers)
1. **DMTN refinancing (CGR 52/53/55/56, ~R173m):** Any failure to refinance or roll the FY27 maturities by **31 August 2026** is a hard red line — would trigger a liquidity event and likely force a deeply discounted capital raise. Monitor SENS for new note issuance (CGR 63+) and lender commitment letters.
2. **BDC infrastructure cost-recovery agreement:** Management states BDC "currently with no finalised infrastructure cost recovery agreement" requires ~R150m to complete Phase 1. **Execution of the public-sector agreement by end-FY27** is the single largest de-risking catalyst; failure extends the cash burn and delays the R21.5bn pipeline.
3. **Contract-asset ECL / "specific exposure" bucket:** The R938.1m "specific exposure" bucket (74% of contract assets) carries a blended ECL that produced only a R4.2m charge. Any **write-down >R50m** (5.9% of the R852m future-cost bucket) would cut NAV by R0.52/share and signal the reclassification was a deterioration, not a relabel.
4. **Net debt/equity vs 0.75:1 internal target:** FY26 0.74:1 is already at the internal ceiling and flattered by R102.1m capitalised interest. A **breach of 0.85:1** (FY27E base case 0.84:1) would pressure the DSCR and likely force an equity raise or asset sales at distressed prices.
5. **CFO turning positive:** FY26 CFO −R14.8m (pre-WC) and −R99.1m (post-WC). **Two consecutive halves of positive CFO** is the minimum evidence of self-funding; without it, the dividend (R8.2m) and debt service remain debt-funded.

### 6.2 Quantified Sensitivity Impacts (rule of thumb)
