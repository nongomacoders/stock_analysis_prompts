### Junction 6: Tax Mechanics, Deferred Tax Liability Surge & Maintenance Provision Reclassification

* **Initial Anomaly (Q16, Q19, Q27, Q41, Q56, Q61):** The Income Statement reports a tax charge of **R47,370,706** (effective tax rate of **22.82%** vs. 27% statutory corporate tax rate), yet Cash Tax Paid on the Cash Flow Statement is only **R3,890,706** (8.21% of P&L tax), while the Deferred Tax Liability (DTL) sits at **R409,486,025** (26.06% of Total Equity). * **Forensic Determination & Mathematical Reconciliation:**
1. **Effective Tax Rate (22.82%) Reconciliation:** Statutory PBT of **R207,600,709** includes **R23,982,406** in "Share of profit of joint ventures – net of tax". Because JV profits are already taxed inside the JV entities prior to equity-accounted recognition, stripping R23,982,406 out of PBT leaves **Pre-JV PBT of R183,618,303**. Calculating the tax rate on Pre-JV PBT yields **25.80%** ($\text{R47,370,706} \div \text{R183,618,303}$; FY2025: $\text{R46,428,106} \div [\text{R212,543,990} - \text{R42,874,266}] = \mathbf{27.36\%}$), reconciling closely to the 27.0% South African statutory tax rate.


2. **The Cash Tax Shield (R3.89m Paid vs. R47.37m Expensed):** Under IFRS 15, Calgro M3 recognises Over-Time construction revenue and capitalised infrastructure assets in advance of legal transfer/cash receipt, whereas the South African Income Tax Act (specifically Section 24C allowances for future expenditure on construction contracts and timing of property transfers) defers the cash tax liability until physical transfer and milestone finalisation. Consequently:


* **Net Deferred Tax Liability Movement:** In FY2026, the DTL increased by **+R43,419,636** (from R366,066,389 to **R409,486,025**, 100% in RPD) while the Deferred Tax Asset (DTA) decreased by **R1,234,593** (from R11,363,028 to **R10,128,435**, 100% in Memorial Parks), creating a **net deferred tax charge of R44,654,229**.


* **Exact Tax Cash Flow Proof:** Total P&L Tax (**R47,370,706**) − Net Deferred Tax Charge (**R44,654,229**) = **Current Income Tax Charge of R2,716,477**. Reconciling to the Balance Sheet tax accounts: Opening Net Current Tax Asset (**R230,441**, being R288,717 asset less R58,276 liability) − Current Tax Charge (**R2,716,477**) + Cash Tax Paid (**R3,890,706**) = **Closing Net Current Tax Asset of R1,503,937** (exactly matching the Balance Sheet: **R1,743,347** current tax asset less **R239,410** current tax liability = **R1,503,937**).




3. **Maintenance Provision Reclassification (`%` Footnote):** In FY2026, management reclassified the Memorial Parks perpetual care obligation (**R66,328,209** in FY2026; **R57,720,206** in FY2025, up **+14.91%**) out of Trade and Other Payables into a separate IAS 37 **Maintenance Provision** line item.
