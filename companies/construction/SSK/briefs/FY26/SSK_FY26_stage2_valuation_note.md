## 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

**Operational & Balance Sheet Red Lines (Thesis-Breaking Triggers).**

1. **Excess billings unwind exceeding R400m in any half-year** — signals project delays or cancellations forcing refunds of client advances; would compress liquidity and invalidate the working capital model. Monitor the contract liability balance in the interim results (November 2026).
2. **Zambian government receivable recovery below 50% of the R74m net carrying value** — would require a further ECL charge and undermine the Africa Region turnaround narrative. Monitor SENS and the FY27 interim ECL note.
3. **South African road infrastructure receivable (R121.6m over-180-day bucket) moving to legal recovery** — a write-down would hit HEPS by approximately 58 cents and signal private-sector payment deterioration.
4. **Standard Bank facility covenant breach or renegotiation** — leverage above 2.0x, interest cover below 3.0x, or debt service cover below 2.0x would trigger a re-rating to distress. Current headroom is wide (0.79x, 6.34x, 4.66x) but covenants tighten annually on 1 March.
5. **Performance guarantee call-up exceeding R200m** — the R4.08bn guarantee book is the largest off-balance-sheet tail; a single large call-up would consume the R1.1bn cash buffer.
6. **Order book decline below R12bn** — would break the 1.5x revenue cover threshold and signal award momentum stalling.
