### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **p.21 `[^1]`** — "Other expenses and income exclude intra-Group management fees. Finance income and finance costs exclude intra-Group interest." `[D006.2, p. 21]`. This means the segment PBT lines are **before** intra-group management fee allocations and intra-group interest; the second block of the table (management fees, finance income/(costs), dividend eliminations) reconciles segment PBT to group PBT of 356,911. Implication: segment margins computed from the first block overstate true segment profitability for segments that are net payers of management fees (Barberton −5,687, Evander −2,831, Tennant −3,188) and understate for Corporate (+12,860 received). **Verification needed**: confirm in D008 whether APM segment costs (e.g., AISC by operation) are stated before or after these intra-group allocations.

- **p.22 `[^1]`** — "The segment assets and liabilities above exclude intra-Group balances." `[D006.2, p. 22]`. Segment net assets (excluding goodwill) sum to 1,017,559; group goodwill 18,438 sits entirely in Barberton. Implication: segment ROIC/asset-turn calculations must add back intra-group funding (Funding Company net assets 137,658; Corporate 43,459) to reconcile to group. **Verification**: check D005.1 balance sheet total equity vs 1,017,559 + 18,438 = 1,035,997.

- **p.22 `[^2]`** — "Capital expenditure comprises additions to property, plant and equipment, mineral rights and exploration assets." `[D006.2, p. 22]`. FY26 capex = US$223,995k vs FY25 US$167,909k (+33%). Segment split FY26: Barberton 40,081; Evander 70,941; Solar 7,159; MTR 32,599; Tennant 71,304; Corporate 1,833. Implication: **Tennant capex of US$71,304k in FY26 is 32% of group capex** — this is the Tennant expansion (crusher/mill/filter per Q16) and must be checked against FY27 capex guidance. Solar capex 7,159 despite disposal on 30 June 2026 — confirm no FY27 solar capex.

- **p.22 `[^3]` vs p.24 `[^4]` — Adjusted EBITDA definition inconsistency.** FY26 definition adjusts only for impairment losses on non-financial assets `[D006.2, p. 22]`; FY25 definition also adjusts for bargain purchase gains and unrealised fair value losses on derivatives `[D006.2, p. 24]`. Implication: FY26 vs FY25 Adjusted EBITDA (609,431 vs 226,584) is **not like-for-like** — FY25 includes a −28,019 bargain purchase gain reversal and +1,925 derivative loss reversal that FY26 does not. **Verification**: confirm in D008 which definition is used for covenant/APM purposes and restate FY25 on FY26 basis (FY25 EBITDA 249,724 + impairment 2,954 = 252,678 on FY26 definition, vs reported Adjusted EBITDA 226,584).

- **p.23 `[^1]`** — "Tennant Mines includes Tennant company and Yungatha. Tennant company was acquired in November 2024 and the results are for an eight-month period. Yungatha was acquired in December 2024 and the results are for a seven-month period." `[D006.2, p. 23]`. Implication: **FY25 Tennant revenue of only US$3,873k and PBT US$30,396k (including 28,019 bargain gain) is a partial-period, non-annualised figure**. FY26 Tennant revenue 132,804 and PBT 23,836 are the first full-year comparatives. Any FY27 Tennant forecast must be built off FY26, not FY25. **Verification**: confirm in D009 the Tennant FY26 production/grade and FY27 guidance to anchor the run-rate.

- **p.24 Geographical information** — Non-current assets: South Africa 911,962 (FY25 740,819); Australia 365,207 (FY25 155,716); Other 480. `[D006.2, p. 24]`. Implication: **Australia non-current assets more than doubled (+US$209m)** — this is Tennant expansion capex plus Emmerson assets. Confirms the Australian asset base is now material (28.6% of group non-current assets) and must be depreciated in FY27 forecasts. **Verification**: check PPE note for Australian depreciation run-rate and Emmerson asset fair value.

- **Solar projects disposal** — "The solar plants were sold at arm's length to the respective mines on 30 June 2026. Following completion of the disposal, the Group no longer has any operating activities or assets attributable to this segment and the segment will no longer be separately reported from 1 July 2026." `[D006.2, p. 20]`. Implication: FY26 Solar segment (revenue nil, loss −2,448 pre-tax, capex 7,159, net assets −6,962) **disappears from FY27**. The intra-group sale means the mines now bear the solar electricity cost directly — check whether FY27 electricity cost per segment steps up. **Verification**: confirm in D008/D009 whether the solar disposal creates any FY27 gain/loss on disposal or changes electricity cost lines.
