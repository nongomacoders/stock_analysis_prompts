### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

- **Note 4 footnote [^1] — "Based on customer country of origin." [D006.3, p. 25]**
  - This qualifies the geographical revenue split (South Africa 1,023,299; Australia 132,804; UK/Europe 328; US 20). It means the split reflects **where the customer is domiciled, not where the mine is located**. Implication for forecasting: the Australia 132,804 line is a customer-market attribution (likely Tennant offtake to an Australian refiner/buyer), not a proxy for Australian asset revenue. Do not use this table to allocate revenue by producing asset — use D006.2 segment analysis for that. Verification: cross-check Australia 132,804 against Tennant segment revenue in D006.2 and D009.

- **Contract liability narrative (unfootnoted but commentary-bearing) [D006.3, p. 25]**
  - Two FY26 advance receipts totalling 8,030 (Barberton RMB forward US$4.5m + Tennant US$3.5m), **both settled in physical bullion in July 2026** (i.e., FY27). This is a critical phasing point: the cash was received in FY26 but the revenue/ounces will be recognised in FY27. Forecast implication: FY27 gold revenue includes ~8,030 of ounces delivered against FY26 prepayments with **no matching FY27 cash inflow** — a working-capital drag in FY27 and a reason FY27 revenue may exceed FY27 cash receipts. Also confirms the prior 24-month RMB forward (4,846oz/month @ ZAR1,025,000/kg ≈ US$1,723/oz) was fully fulfilled in FY25 — no residual hedged ounces overhang into FY27. Verification: check D009/D008 that July 2026 deliveries are counted in FY27 ounces and that no double-count occurs against FY27 production guidance.

- **Note 5 — "Borrowing costs capitalised – FY26 nil vs FY25 7,190" [D006.4, p. 25]**
  - This is a material qualification on the borrowings cost line: FY25 gross borrowing cost was effectively 25,033 − 7,190 = 17,843 net of capitalisation; FY26 is 14,505 with **no capitalisation**. The 42% YoY fall in the borrowings line (25,033→14,505) is therefore partly a capitalisation-cessation effect, not purely deleveraging. Forecast implication: FY27 finance cost run-rate should be anchored on the FY26 14,505 (no capitalisation assumed) unless new project capex (Tennant crusher/mill/filter, Soweto Cluster) triggers renewed capitalisation. Verification: check D003/D008 for any FY27 capitalised interest guidance and debt balances.

- **Note 5 — Environmental rehabilitation obligation finance cost (2,025) [D006.4, p. 25]**
  - Non-cash accretion; stable YoY (2,156→2,025). Retain as a recurring non-cash finance cost in FY27 model; do not treat as cash interest.

- **Note 5 — "Fair value gain on short-term investments 1,142 (Note 13)" [D006.4, p. 25]**
