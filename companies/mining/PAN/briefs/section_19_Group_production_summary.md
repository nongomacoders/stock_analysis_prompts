### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

**No footnote markers (`[^1]`, `*`, note references) are present in the supplied D009 evidence** (pp. 48–50). The tables are presented without footnote definitions or small-print qualifications in this capture.

However, several **embedded table qualifications and structural anomalies** function as de facto caveats and must be flagged:

1. **"Head grade – excluding third-party buy-in feedstock" row** [D009, p.48]: This is a critical qualification. Evander's headline total head grade is **4.8g/t**, but excluding third-party buy-in feedstock it is **11.2g/t**. The 202,555t of third-party buy-in at Evander (vs 34,411t FY25) massively dilutes the reported grade and drives the 4.6g/t overall recovered grade. **Implication**: Evander's underground grade quality (>11g/t) is masked by toll-milling/buy-in activity. For forecasting, the underground grade is the sustainable metric; the buy-in feedstock is a margin/volume variable. Must verify in D003/D006.2 whether buy-in feedstock is expected to continue at FY26 levels in FY27.

2. **"US$ all-in sustaining cost – excluding third-party buy-in feedstock" row** [D009, p.49]: Group AISC excluding buy-in = **US$1,798/oz** vs headline **US$1,867/oz**. This confirms buy-in feedstock **raises** group AISC by ~US$69/oz (i.e., buy-in is margin-dilutive at current prices). Evander AISC excluding buy-in = **US$1,698/oz** vs headline **US$1,914/oz** — a US$216/oz difference. **Implication**: If buy-in feedstock volumes fall in FY27, group AISC should improve materially. Must verify FY27 buy-in assumptions in D003/D006.2.

3. **Tennant FY25 AIC of US$32,041/oz** [D009, p.49]: This is a nonsensical-looking figure driven by the 1,513oz ramp-up year against fixed costs. It confirms FY26 (US$4,339/oz) is the first meaningful full-year AIC. **Implication**: FY25 comparatives for Tennant are not forecast-relevant; use FY26 as the base.

4. **Tennant "surface feedstock" vs "tailings" classification** [D009, p.48–49]: Tennant's 751,914t is classified as **surface feedstock** (not tailings), processed at 94% recovery — a different processing route from the SA tailings operations (33–47% recovery). **Implication**: Tennant's cost/grade/recovery profile is structurally different; do not blend with SA tailings metrics.

5. **Capex total discrepancy**: D009 group capex = **US$214.9m** [D009, p.50] vs stored FY26 total of **224.0** [D008.3, p.44]. A ~US$9.1m gap exists — likely corporate/exploration capex not allocated to operations in D009. Must reconcile in D006.5/D008.3.

6. **Revenue table anomaly**: D009 shows Barberton Mines total revenue ZAR 5,958.6m but the "Mining operations Barberton" column shows 5,052.1 and Evander 3,397.5 — the "Barberton Mines total" (5,958.6) appears to include BTRP (906.5) but the arithmetic 5,052.1 + 906.5 = 5,958.6 confirms this. Similarly Evander Mines total 7,452.6 = 3,397.5 + 4,055.1 (Elikhulu). **Implication**: The "Mines total" columns are additive roll-ups including surface operations; do not double-count.

7. **Gold produced total rounding**: Underground produced 105,479oz but group total shows 105,478oz [D009, p.48] — 1oz rounding difference. Immaterial but noted.
