### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

**Footnote [^1] on p. 40 (FY26 table):** *"This total may not reflect the sum of the line items due to rounding."*
- **What it changes**: The group AISC of 508.4 and AIC of 703.4 are subject to rounding; the underlying sum of by-mine figures (181.4+90.2+20.1+70.3+73.8+72.6 = 508.4) ties, but individual line-item sums within columns may differ by ±0.1. For forecasting, use the by-mine figures as the primary build and treat the group total as a check, not a plug. [D008.2, p. 40]

**Footnote [^1] on p. 41 (FY25 table):** *"The above table was disclosed in ZAR million in the 2024 integrated annual report."*
- **What it changes**: The FY25 comparative has been **re-presented from ZAR to US$** using the FY25 average rate of 18.17. This means FY25 US$ figures are not directly comparable to the originally published ZAR figures and may differ from any prior-year US$ disclosure. For YoY analysis, the FX translation itself (18.17→16.90) is a material driver of the US$ AISC increase — roughly 7% of the US$1,600→1,867/oz move is pure FX. Must verify against the 2024 IAR ZAR figures if a clean constant-currency comparison is needed. [D008.2, p. 41]

**Footnote [^1] on p. 42 (FY26 unit table):** *"This total may not reflect the sum of the line items due to rounding."*
- **What it changes**: Same rounding caveat applies to the per-unit (US$/oz, ZAR/kg, A$/kg) table. Note the **A$/kg column for Tennant** uses a separate A$ cost base (A$81.0m cost of production, A$106.8m AISC, A$205.1m AIC) translated at 0.68 — this is the only operation with a non-ZAR functional currency and must be modelled separately. [D008.2, p. 42]

**Footnote [^1] on p. 43 (FY25 unit table):** *"This total may not reflect the sum of the line items due to rounding."*
- **What it changes**: Same caveat. Critically, the FY25 Tennant AIC of **US$33,693/oz** (and A$1,666,455/kg) is an artefact of a near-zero production base (37kg / 1,179oz) against A$61.1m AIC — this is **not a meaningful run-rate** and must be excluded from any trend analysis. FY26 Tennant AIC of US$4,339/oz is the first meaningful full-year figure. [D008.2, p. 43]

**Embedded qualification on p. 40 (AIC definition):** *"AIC starts with AISC and adds additional costs which relate to the growth of the Group, including non-sustaining capital expenditure not associated with current operations and costs such as voluntary severance pay."*
- **What it changes**: The AISC→AIC bridge (508.4→703.4, +195.0) is **entirely expansion capex** in FY26 — no voluntary severance line appears (unlike FY25's 1.4). This means FY26 AIC is a "growth-heavy" number and the 195.0 expansion capex is the key FY27 forecast lever. If expansion capex steps down in FY27 (e.g., Tennant build completion), AIC should compress toward AISC. [D008.2, pp. 40–41]

**Embedded qualification on p. 40 (FX methodology):** *"The US$ equivalent is converted at the average exchange rate applicable for the current reporting period as disclosed in the Group's operational production table on pages 92 to 97."*
- **What it changes**: The US$ AISC is a **translation of ZAR/A$ costs at the period average**, not a transaction-level rate. FY27 US$ AISC will therefore move with the ZAR/A$ average even if local-currency costs are flat. The 16.90 (FY26) vs 18.17 (FY25) move alone accounts for a meaningful chunk of the US$ AISC increase. Pages 92–97 are outside this capture — flag for D009. [D008.2, p. 40]
