### 2. Footnote & Qualification Deep-Dive

**Footnote `[^1]` — MTR calcine constraint** `[D002, physical PDF p. 4]`
> "Expected production from MTR takes into account treatment of final calcine elements, whereafter annual production is forecast to increase to over 60,000oz per year."

- **What it changes:** The FY27 MTR guidance of 49,000–54,000oz is explicitly a *transitional, calcine-constrained* year, not a steady-state run-rate. The H2 weighting already visible in the table (H1 19,000–22,000oz vs H2 30,000–32,000oz) is the mechanical signature of calcine depletion mid-year. Post-FY27, MTR is guided to **>60,000oz/yr** — a step-change of roughly +10–20% above the FY27 midpoint.
- **Hidden constraint / step-change:** The forecast must model MTR as a two-phase asset: constrained FY27, then a step-up in FY28. The exact quarter in which calcine treatment ends is not stated and is the single most important timing variable for FY27 H2 and FY28.
- **Verification required:** Confirm in `D003`/`D008`/`D009` the quarter of calcine exhaustion, the throughput/grade assumed to reach >60koz, and whether the >60koz is a FY28 exit-rate or full-year FY28 figure.

**Embedded qualification — AISC at top of guidance** `[D002, physical PDF p. 2–3]`
> "AISC of US$1,867/oz … within cost guidance for FY26 of between US$1,820/oz and US$1,870/oz … A strengthened US$/ZAR exchange rate of US$/ZAR:16.90 compared to guidance of US$/ZAR:18.50, which adversely impacted unit costs."

- FY26 AISC landed at the **very top** of the guidance band (US$1,867 vs ceiling US$1,870), despite the weaker-than-guided ZAR being the stated headwind. This implies underlying ZAR cost inflation was largely absorbed but not beaten — a warning for FY27.
- **Verification required:** Reconcile the FY26 AISC bridge (FX, inflation, Tennant ramp, third-party material, share-based payments, royalties) in `D008`.

**Embedded qualification — "lower-cost operations" exclusion** `[D002, physical PDF p. 2–3]`
> "Lower-cost operations, which account for 90.8% of annual production, achieved an AISC of US$1,702/oz … These low-cost operations exclude only Barberton Mines' Sheba and Consort Mines."

- Arithmetic implication: Group AISC × total oz = US$1,867 × 272,310 = **US$508.4m**. Lower-cost ops = US$1,702 × (272,310 × 0.908 = 247,257oz) = **US$420.8m**. Residual = **US$87.6m over ~25,053oz = ~US$3,497/oz** implied AISC for Sheba + Consort.
- FY25 equivalent: US$1,600 × 196,527 = US$314.4m; lower-cost US$1,434 × 169,406 = US$242.9m; residual US$71.5m / 27,121oz = **~US$2,637/oz**. So the excluded high-cost operations' implied AISC rose ~33% YoY — a material drag hidden inside the "90.8%" headline.
- **Verification required:** Confirm Sheba/Consort actual AISC and production in `D008`/`D009`; confirm whether these are being wound down or rehabilitated.

**Embedded qualification — FY27 AISC guidance vs FY26 actual** `[D002, physical PDF p. 3]`
> "Group AISC guidance for FY27 is between US$2,075/oz and US$2,175/oz (assuming an exchange rate of US$/ZAR:17.00), allowing for above-inflation increases for reagents, electricity and other key inputs."

- This is a **+11% to +16% step-up** vs FY26 actual US$1,867, *despite* (i) higher production guidance (280–302koz vs 272koz) and (ii) a *weaker* ZAR assumption (17.00 vs 16.90 actual), which should be a USD-cost tailwind of ~0.6%. Restating FY26 at 17.00 gives ~US$1,856/oz, so the underlying ZAR cost inflation embedded in guidance is ~12–17%.
- **Verification required:** A quantified cost bridge in `D003`/`D008` — how much is Tennant ramp/White Devil strip, MTR calcine, reagent/electricity inflation, and share-based payments.

**Embedded qualification — dividend share-count tension** `[D002, physical PDF p. 2–3]`
- Final dividend ZAR 1,583.6m ÷ ZAR 0.65/share = **~2,436.3m shares**.
- Interim dividend ZAR 280.0m ÷ ZAR 0.12/share = **~2,333.3m shares**.
- Weighted average shares in issue = **2,029.6m** (confirmed by EPS: 17.60c × 2,029.6m = US$357.2m = attributable earnings; HEPS 17.64c × 2,029.6m = US$358.0m = headline earnings).
- **Tension:** The dividend-implied share count (~2,436m) is ~20% above the weighted average (2,029.6m), and even the March 2026 interim implies ~2,333m — *above* the full-year weighted average, which is arithmetically impossible if shares only increased. Possible explanations: dividend declared on shares in issue at a later record date (post-Emmerson scrip, concluded 22 June 2026), treasury/BEE shares, or a misstatement. **This must be resolved before modelling DPS, dilution and NAV/share.**
- **Verification required:** Share capital note in `D006` and NAV/share reconciliation in `D008`.

**Embedded qualification — Soweto Cluster DFS economics** `[D002, physical PDF p. 3]`
> "estimated capital cost of US$216 million, a post-tax NPV13 of ~US$109 million and a payback period of less than three years at current gold prices … (at US$/ZAR:17.00)."

- NPV13 (US$109m) is only ~50% of capex (US$216m) — a modest return at the stated 13% discount rate, yet payback <3 years "at current gold prices" implies a very high near-term margin. The two are only reconcilable with a specific (unstated) gold price deck and mine schedule. **The price deck is not disclosed here.**
- **Verification required:** Gold price and discount assumptions in `D003`/`D008`; confirm FID timing (Dec 2026) and funding.

**Embedded qualification — renewable mix declined** `[D002, physical PDF p. 4]`
> "renewable energy mix of 8.1% (FY25: 8.8%) … on track to achieve a 15% Group renewable energy mix by FY27 and more than 70% by FY30."

- The mix *fell* YoY despite US$5.1m solar savings — consistent with total energy consumption growing faster (MTR expansion, Evander). The 15% FY27 target requires a near-doubling; the Tennant 6.3MWAC plant only reaches first power Feb 2027 and MTR's 19.0MWAC contractor is not yet appointed (board approval pending). **Execution risk on the FY27 renewable target.**
- **Verification required:** Renewable generation/capacity schedule and PPA (NOA Group) timing in `D003`/`D008`.
