### Core Institutional Investment Thesis

Pan African Resources PLC (JSE: PAN / LSE: PAF / ASX: PAF) has completed a structural transformation from a South African underground miner into a diversified, multi-jurisdictional mid-tier producer. In FY26, Group gold output rose **38.6%** to a record **272,310 oz** while the average realised gold price surged **54.8%** to **US$4,235/oz** (**ZAR 2,308,329/kg**). This operational and pricing inflection drove a **259.6%** surge in net operating cash flow to **US$557.0m**, swinging the balance sheet by **US$336.2m** from **US$150.5m** Net Debt in FY25 to **US$185.7m** Net Cash (**US$185,597k** exact) at 30 June 2026.

Despite this operational inflection, consensus models and surface-level screens misprice PAN due to four forensic accounting distortions that artificially depress reported earnings, inflate unit costs, and obscure forward Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE):

1. **Underlying Recurring EBITDA is US$648.5m (Not US$609.4m):** Management's reported Adjusted EBITDA of **US$609.4m** (`D006.2`, Note 3, p. 41) absorbs **100%** of a **US$57,272k** (**US$57.3m**) P&L charge for cash-settled Share-Based Payments (SBP) (`D006.9`, Note 17.1, p. 63). Because the 90-day VWAP share price nearly tripled (+195%) from **ZAR 10.40** to **ZAR 30.69** (`D006.9`, Note 17.3, p. 64), IFRS 2 Monte Carlo and binomial option revaluations triggered a one-off **US$39.1m** non-cash liability build on top of the **US$18.2m** actual cash SBP settled. Adding back the non-recurring mark-to-market spike reveals a **Cash-Normalised Recurring EBITDA of US$648.5m** (and **US$666.7m** pre-SBP).


2. **True Organic AISC is US$1,790/oz (and US$1,702/oz Across 90.8% of Output):** Headline Group AISC of **US$1,867/oz** (and FY27E guidance of **US$2,075–2,175/oz** at US$/ZAR 17.00) is optically inflated by two items:


* **Third-Party Buy-In Feedstock (+US$77/oz Group Distortion):** PAN processed **18,667 oz** (**6.85%** of total production) of third-party surface and artisanal feedstock across Barberton (**4,337 oz**), BTRP (**1,958 oz**), Evander (**6,609 oz**), and MTR (**5,763 oz**). Purchased at ~60–70% of spot gold plus high-reagent milling costs (carrying an average AISC of ~**US$2,910/oz**), this stream generated **~US$24.7m** in incremental risk-free cash flow using spare mill capacity, but mathematically inflated Group AISC by **+US$77/oz** (and Evander Underground AISC by **+US$169/oz** from **US$1,745/oz** organic to **US$1,914/oz** reported). *(Forensic Verification Note: We explicitly note that the primary Group Production Summary table in the full PDF on page 95 prints "US$ all-in sustaining cost excluding third-party buy-in feedstock" at **US$1,798/oz** for the Group [distorting Group AISC by +US$69/oz] and US$1,698/oz** for Evander Mines [distorting Evander AISC by +US$216/oz][cite: 50]; both our Stage 1 bottom-up derivation of US$1,790/oz and the page 95 table of **US$1,798/oz** confirm that true organic AISC sits below US$1,800/oz).*


* **Sheba & Consort Structural Drag (+US$165/oz Group Distortion):** Barberton's mature Sheba (**17,033 oz** produced) and Consort (**7,287 oz** produced) shafts sold **25,154 oz** at an AISC of **US$3,487/oz** (**US$87.7m** total AISC, p. 75). Excluding Sheba and Consort, the remaining **90.8%** of Group sales (**247,219 oz**, including Fairview Underground at **US$2,085/oz**) achieved an AISC of **US$1,702/oz**.




3. **The Capex Fork & Section 36 Tax Shield Paradox:** Management guides **US$330.0m** of capital expenditure in FY27E and **US$303.0m** in FY28E (p. 15)—a **US$633.0m** two-year reinvestment programme that is **47% higher** than FY26 peak construction capex of **US$224.0m**. Under **Section 36(11) of the South African Income Tax Act**, 100% of qualifying mining capex is deductible upfront against taxable mining income in the year incurred. Analysts who deduct management's **US$330m/US$303m** expansion capex budget while simultaneously applying the **33.2% FY26 Accounting ETR** as a cash tax outflow commit a **~US$85m–US$95m per annum double-counting error**. Conversely, if gold prices moderate or uncommitted expansion projects are deferred (**Scenario B: Capex Cliff**), capex drops to **US$110.0m** in FY27E and **US$65.0m** in FY28E, unlocking **+US$325.0m in incremental after-tax FCFE** over 24 months even after Cash Tax steps up from **13.9%** to **~29.5%** of PBT.


4. **Definitive Economic Share Count & Reciprocal Treasury Shield:** While management's dividend notice cites **2,434,309,216** shares in issue (which double-deducted the **2,003,735** FY26 share buyback from the true gross register of **2,436,312,950** post-Emmerson shares), **306,358,058** shares (**12.57%** of post-Emmerson gross capital) are held in treasury by **PAR Gold Proprietary Limited**. ## 3. Consolidated Financial Statements & FCFE Bridge (Scenario A vs. Scenario B)

### Key P&L, Tax & Cash Flow Modeling Mechanics

1. **Revenue:** Modelled at **291,000 oz** $\times$ **US$4,600/oz** = **US$1,338.6m** gold revenue plus **US$4.4m** silver/copper/other = **US$1,343.0m** in FY27E. In FY28E, at **US$4,900/oz**, Scenario A (**325,000 oz**) generates **US$1,597.5m** total revenue, while Scenario B (**301,000 oz**, reflecting deferred expansion ounces at Tennant, MTR, and Royal Sheba) generates **US$1,479.9m**.


2. **SBP Normalisation vs. FY27 Cash Settlement Drain (Junction 3):**
* *P&L Charge:* Following the tripling of the share price to **ZAR 30.69** in FY26, P&L SBP normalises to a steady-state **US$18.0m/yr** accrual in FY27E and FY28E (vs. **US$57.3m** in FY26A).


* *Cash Flow Working Capital Impact:* At 30 June 2026, current SBP liabilities stood at **US$43,969k** (`D005.1`, p. 30; Note 17.1, p. 63) for vesting PAR Gold G/I and PASA tranches. Consequently, while the FY27E P&L charge is **US$18.0m**, actual cash SBP settled in FY27E is modelled at **US$44.0m** (a net **-US$26.0m** cash working capital outflow in FY27E), normalising to **US$18.0m** cash paid in FY28E.




3. **Section 36 Cash Tax vs. Deferred Tax Split (Junctions 6 & 7):**
* In FY27E, Pre-Tax Profit (PBT) is **US$694.5m**. Under the SA Gold Formula ($Y = 33 - 165/X$, where $X \approx 52\% \implies Y \approx 29.8\%$) and Australian corporate tax (**30.0%**), total accounting tax expense at ~29.5% ETR is **US$204.9m**.


* **Scenario A (Expedited Growth Capex: US$330.0m in FY27E / US$303.0m in FY28E):** Deducting **US$330.0m** of capex upfront under SA Section 36 and Australian accelerated mining depreciation shields ~**US$110.0m** of cash taxes in FY27E, leaving **Cash Tax Paid at US$95.0m** (**13.7% of PBT**) and **Non-Cash Deferred Tax at US$109.9m**. In FY28E, **US$303.0m** of capex keeps **Cash Tax Paid at US$105.0m** (**12.0% of PBT**).


* **Scenario B (Committed Capex Cliff: US$110.0m in FY27E / US$65.0m in FY28E):** With historical UCR shields at MTR and Evander exhausted in FY26 and Tennant's assessed losses down to **US$3k** (Note 6.2, p. 48), dropping capex to **US$110.0m** in FY27E causes **Cash Tax Paid to step up to US$160.0m** (**23.0% of PBT**), and in FY28E (**US$65.0m** capex) **Cash Tax Paid converges to US$215.0m** (**27.2% of PBT**).

Our institutional target price of **R43.50/share** reflects an equal 50/50 probability weighting of both trajectories.

## 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts ("What to Watch For That Will Move the Stock")

### 1. Operational & Production Red Lines (Downside Triggers to Target Price)

Portfolio managers should monitor four operational red lines that would invalidate our base-case margin expansion and trigger a target price downgrade:

* **Red Line 1 — Group Organic AISC Breaching US$1,950/oz (or Reported AISC Exceeding US$2,175/oz Upper Guidance):** If South African wage settlements, Eskom tariff hikes, or cyanide prepayments push organic AISC (ex-buy-in ore) above **US$1,950/oz** at US$/ZAR 17.00, it signals structural cost leakage beyond third-party feedstock math.


* **Red Line 2 — Sheba & Consort Failing to Exit Cash-Burn Territory (>US$3,200/oz AISC in FY27H2):** Sheba and Consort burned margin at **US$3,487/oz** in FY26. If Western Cross LHOS stoping and PC Shaft dewatering below 48 Level fail to lift combined Sheba/Consort sales back above **13,000 oz per half-year** and compress AISC below **US$3,200/oz** by FY27H2, management will face pressure to place Consort or marginal Sheba sections on care and maintenance (impairing SOTP by **~R0.68/share**).

* **Red Line 4 — Tennant Mines White Devil Ramp-Up Slippage (<22,000 oz in FY27H1):** Following FY26's slower ramp-up (**32,124 oz**), Tennant Mines must deliver **22,000–24,000 oz** in FY27H1 and **26,000–28,000 oz** in FY27H2.
