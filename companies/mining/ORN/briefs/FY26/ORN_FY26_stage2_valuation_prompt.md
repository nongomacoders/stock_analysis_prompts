A forensic reconciliation of the sequential working papers against the technical tables in Section D009 (`Ore Reserves and Mineral Resources Statement`) and funding disclosures in Section D007 (`CFO Review`) reveals significant structural qualifications that materially alter the economic reality:

Prieska remained in the "pre-execution phase" with conditions precedent (CPs) on the Glencore facility still outstanding, while Okiep remained in an "optimisation phase" with its headline **A$75m NPV** explicitly flagged as stale and pre-optimisation.


2. **Severe Structural Funding Gap at Prieska:** Management’s headline **US$250m** Glencore prepayment facility is strictly tranched (**US$40m** for Uppers, **US$210m** for Deeps, with a conditional **US$50m** early-works sub-limit) and does not cover Prieska's **A$578m** peak capital requirement. #### Junction 4: Unit Costs (AISC) & By-Product Credit Sensitivity

* **Initial Claim (`D003`, p. 15; `D004`, p. 16):** Prieska is marketed at a headline **"US$0.94/lb AISC*"**, placing it in the **"Lowest 25% Global cost quartile of copper producers."**

* **Forensic Reconciliation (`D008.1`, p. 23; `D008.2`, p. 25; `D009.3`, p. 31):**
* **The By-Product Credit Mechanism:** While `D003` p. 15 merely defines the asterisk as `* All-in sustaining costs`, `D008.1` p. 23 reveals the critical second footnote: **`** Assumes Zn revenue is a by-product credit`**.


* **Scale of Zinc Offset:** Over the 13.2-year LOM, Prieska is scheduled to produce **610,630t of contained zinc** versus **213,055t of contained copper** (a **2.87 : 1** zinc-to-copper physical tonnage ratio; at steady state, **65,000 tpa Zn** vs **22,000 tpa Cu**).


* **Price Deck & NSR Basis (`D009.3`, p. 31, Footnote 4):** The March 2025 DFS reserve equivalency formula utilizes consensus long-term metal prices (December 2024) of **US$8,900/t Cu** (**~US$4.04/lb**) and **US$2,450/t Zn** (**~US$1.11/lb**), alongside Net Smelter Return (NSR) factors of **101.3% for Cu** (reflecting precious metal credits exceeding treatment charges) and **69.7% for Zn** (reflecting heavy zinc smelter treatment charges/deductions), and plant recoveries of **85.5% Cu** and **82.1% Zn**:



$$\text{1\% Zn} = \frac{2,450 \times 69.7\% \times 82.1\%}{8,900 \times 101.3\% \times 85.5\%} = \frac{1,401.97}{7,708.40} = 0.1819\% \text{ Cu (reported as } 0.185\% \text{ Cu)}$$



* Because total LOM gross zinc revenue (**610.6kt Zn** at **US$2,450/t** = **~US$1.50bn** gross, or **~US$1.04bn** net of the 69.7% NSR factor) is credited entirely against operating costs before dividing by payable copper pounds (**~469.7M lbs** contained Cu), the **US$0.94/lb Cu AISC** is heavily depressed by roughly **~US$2.20/lb** in net zinc credits.
* **Okiep Unit Cost Contrast (`D008.2`, p. 25):** By contrast, Okiep—which is a pure copper project with no zinc by-product credit—reports a March 2025 DFS AISC of **US$2.39/lb Cu** (**2.54x** Prieska's headline unit cost).


* **Currency Exposure (`D002`, p. 9; `D007`, p. 21):** Operating costs at both mines are predominantly ZAR-denominated, whereas concentrate revenues and AISC metrics are reported in USD, leaving the US$0.94/lb figure fully exposed to USD/ZAR exchange rate fluctuations.




* **Final Determination:** Prieska’s **US$0.94/lb Cu AISC** is a **by-product-netted metric**, not a co-product cost. It cannot be compared directly against pure-play copper producers or co-product cost curves without adjusting for zinc price sensitivity (where a 10% drop in zinc prices or increase in zinc TC/RCs mechanically inflates Prieska's reported copper AISC). Okiep's **US$2.39/lb Cu** represents the true standalone copper unit cost structure in the region.

## REQUIRED SECTIONS OF THE INSTITUTIONAL VALUATION NOTE

1. **Executive Investment Thesis & 12-Month JSE Price Target (ZAR/share):**
   - Must begin with a standardized **Executive Valuation Summary Card** containing:
     * **Sector:** [Sector]
     * **Current Reference Share Price:** R[X.XX] (or ZAR cents)
     * **12-Month JSE Target Price:** **R[X.XX]** (or ZAR cents)
     * **Implied Capital Upside:** [+/-X.X%]
     * **Forward Dividend Yield (FY27E):** [X.X%]
     * **Projected 12-Month Total Shareholder Return (TSR):** **[+/-X.X%]**
     * **Institutional Recommendation:** **[BUY | HOLD | SELL]**
   - Followed by the core institutional thesis and operational highlights.
2. **Detailed Asset-by-Asset Operational Forecast (FY27E & FY28E):** Throughput, recovered grade, production (oz), cash costs, and AISC per asset.
3. **Consolidated Financial Statements & FCFE Bridge:** Revenue, EBITDA, Section 36 Cash Tax, Net Profit, and Free Cash Flow to Equity.
4. **Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) DCF Valuation Table:** Asset-by-asset NAV, unallocated corporate costs, net cash/debt, and per-share equity value in ZAR.
5. **Forward Valuation Multiples & Sensitivity Matrix:** Target P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, and 2D sensitivity table (Gold Price vs USD/ZAR).
6. **Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts ('What to Watch For That Will Move the Stock'):**
   - **Operational & Production Red Lines:** Explicitly list 3-5 specific triggers (e.g. AISC cost breaches, underground grade dilution, water/power disruptions) that would lower the target price.
   - **Quantified Sensitivity Impacts:** Exact quantified rule of thumb for each catalyst (e.g. 'Every $50/oz move in gold price or R0.50 move in USD/ZAR impacts FCFE by R X million and fair value by R Y/share').
   - **Expansion & Delivery Catalysts:** Specific milestones required to trigger an upgrade (e.g. MTR / Soweto tailings commercial commissioning, Section 36 capex cliff release).
   - **Upcoming Milestones & Reporting Dates:** Key calendar dates (e.g. Interim results date, production reports, SENS announcements) for portfolio managers to mark.
