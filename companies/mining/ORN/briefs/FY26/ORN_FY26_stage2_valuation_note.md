### Core Institutional Thesis & Forensic SENS Integration (19 October 2026 Release)

Following Orion Minerals' landmark SENS/ASX announcement on **19 October 2026** confirming that the **US$40 million (R720m / A$62.5m) Glencore Tranche A Prepayment Facility is now unconditional** with first drawdown scheduled by the **end of October 2026**, we **upgrade our institutional recommendation from SELL to HOLD** and raise our 12-month risked JSE Target Price by **+23.8% (+5.0 ZAR cents per share)** from **R0.21/share (21.0c)** to **R0.26/share (26.0 ZAR cents | A$0.0226/share)**.

#### 1. What the 19 October 2026 SENS Release Resolves (Positive Valuation Catalysts)

* **Unconditional Financial Close of Glencore Tranche A (US$40m):** All outstanding concentrate offtake and intercreditor agreements (specifically the *pari passu* first-ranking security arrangement between Glencore and Triple Flag Precious Metals) have been executed, and all Conditions Precedent (CPs) for Tranche A have been satisfied.


* **Imminent Cash Inflow & Contractor Execution (October 2026):** First drawdown of the US$40m Tranche A facility is expected by the **end of October 2026** (ahead of our prior November 2026 Scenario A assumption). This enables immediate execution of contracts with **Torque Africa** (geotechnical and final mine design drilling) and **ENPROTEC** (delivery of the **20,000 tpm Uppers Concentrator** on a Build-Own-Operate-Transfer [B-O-O-T] basis), as well as construction of the accommodation village.


* **Favorable 18-Month Interest Capitalisation Holiday:** The SENS disclosure reveals a critical cash-flow protective feature: Glencore's market-related return is settled monthly but is **capitalised until approximately 18 months after first production from the Uppers** (i.e., until ~mid-2029), and includes a step-down in the return rate once commercial production is declared. This reduces cash interest outflows during FY27E and FY28E, directly improving near-term Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE).


* **Confirmed Uppers Production Timeline (End of CY2027):** Targeting first production from the Uppers Mine by **the end of 2027 (Q2 FY28)** shifts the commissioning clock from a "floating pre-execution target" to a funded, contracted execution schedule, validating 6 months of commercial ramp-up production in **H2 FY28E (Jan–June 2028)**.


* **Confirmed 100% Offtake & Stockpile Advance Payment Flexibility:** Offtake agreements have been finalised for **100% of Bulk Concentrates from the Uppers for 5 years** (up to total Uppers production) and **100% of Copper and Zinc Concentrates from the Deeps for a minimum of 10 years** at market benchmark TC/RCs, crucially including a **PCZM option for advance sales and payments for stockpiled concentrates** (accelerating working capital conversion during H2 FY28E ramp-up).



#### 2. Why We Stop Short of a BUY Rating (Structural Overhangs Confirmed by the SENS)

Despite the market enthusiasm surrounding Tranche A, a forensic reading of the 19 October 2026 SENS announcement alongside the Audited FY26 Financial Statements highlights five binding structural constraints that cap fair value at **R0.26/share (-8.5% below the R0.284 reference price)**:

1. **Glencore Tranche B (US$210m) Double-Conditionality Revealed:** Page 1 of the SENS explicitly states that the **US$210 million Tranche B** (and its conditional **US$50m Early Works sub-limit**) remains strictly conditional on *"amongst other things, the Deeps being fully funded and **Glencore securing additional third-party funding to support its prepayments under Tranche B**"*. This confirms that Glencore is syndicating/back-leveraging Tranche B rather than funding it unconditionally off its own balance sheet, leaving Prieska's **A$578m (R6.58bn)** peak Deeps funding requirement—and Triple Flag's undrawn **US$80m** stream—unresolved.


2. **Parent Company Guarantee Escalates Corporate Balance Sheet Risk:** Under *"Key Terms of the Facility"* (Page 2), **"Orion will guarantee the obligations of PCZM under the Prepayment Facility"**. Although Orion's direct beneficial equity interest in PCZM is only **53.32%** (and consolidated accounting share is **63.32%**), Orion Minerals Limited (Parent) provides a **100% corporate guarantee** over the US$40m Tranche A debt alongside *pari passu* first-ranking asset security shared with Triple Flag.


3. **Tranche A is Project-Ring-Fenced; Q4 FY27E Parent Equity Raise Remains Mandatory:** The SENS confirms that the **US$40m Tranche A is advanced directly to subsidiary `PCZM` strictly *"to be used for the construction and startup of the Uppers at Prieska"***. It cannot be upstreamed to fund Orion's corporate overhead, Australian administration, or Okiep (NOM) and Jacomynspan exploration commitments. Consequently, the **Note 2.1.3 (p. 92) Going Concern funding deficit** at the parent company level remains active, still requiring a dilutive equity placement in **H2/Q4 FY27E** (though reduced in size from A$18.0m to A$14.0m / R161.3m due to PCZM absorbing site-level overheads from November 2026).


4. **Scenario B ("Disciplined Ring-Fenced Uppers") is Now Confirmed as the Operational Reality:** Because Tranche A (US$40m) is unconditional while Tranche B (US$210m / US$50m early works) remains conditional on full Deeps funding and Glencore third-party syndication, Orion is operationally executing **Scenario B** in H1/H2 FY27E—advancing the Uppers while Deeps construction waits on multi-party syndication.


5. **Unchanged Attributable Ownership (53.32%) & Reserve-to-LOM Deficit:** The SENS confirms ordinary shares on issue remain at **8,954m** (with **724m options**), PCZM beneficial ownership remains **53.32%**, and the Uppers Probable Reserve remains limited to **629kt @ 2.3% Cu (14.5kt contained Cu)**.
