**Executive Investment Thesis:**
Orion Minerals has successfully transitioned from a stalled developer to a funded constructor of its Phase 1 Uppers mine at Prieska, following the 19 October 2026 SENS confirming the US$40m Glencore Tranche A is unconditional. This is a binary de-risking event that removes near-term liquidity risk and validates the project's technical viability to a major counterparty. However, the investment case remains highly speculative and structurally challenged. The company is still pre-revenue with a going concern note, and the path to full-scale production (Deeps) requires a further US$210m, which remains conditional and unfunded. Furthermore, forensic analysis of the JORC tables reveals that over 34% of the LOM copper and 38% of the LOM zinc production is not backed by Probable Reserves, relying on the conversion of Inferred resources. The headline US$0.94/lb AISC is heavily dependent on zinc by-product credits, making it highly sensitive to zinc prices and USD/ZAR volatility. Our valuation reflects a risked sum-of-the-parts, valuing the now-funded Uppers phase but applying a significant discount to the unfunded Deeps and the pre-optimisation Okiep project.

**Structural Headwinds & Tailwinds:**
*   **Tailwinds:** (1) Finalisation of Tranche A funding and imminent drawdown. (2) Strong long-term copper demand outlook. (3) Brownfield nature of Prieska with existing infrastructure. (4) Strategic partnership with Glencore provides offtake and technical validation.
*   **Headwinds:** (1) Massive remaining funding gap for Deeps (US$210m). (2) Reliance on non-reserve resources for LOM production. (3) High sensitivity to zinc prices and ZAR/USD exchange rates. (4) Execution risk in a high-inflation environment. (5) Stale and pre-optimisation Okiep valuation (A$75m NPV).

### 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts

**Operational & Production Red Lines (What will lower the target price):**
1.  **Uppers Ramp-Up Delay:** Any delay in achieving first concentrate beyond Q1 CY2028 or failure to reach nameplate capacity (20ktpm) by mid-FY28.
2.  **AISC Breach:** Sustained Uppers AISC above US$2.80/lb, indicating cost overruns or lower-than-expected grades.
3.  **Deeps Funding Failure:** Inability to secure the remaining US$210m for Deeps within 12-18 months, which would strand a significant portion of the asset's value.
4.  **Resource Conversion Failure:** Inability to convert Inferred resources to Probable Reserves, which would shorten the mine life and undermine the LOM plan.
5.  **Zinc Price Collapse:** A sustained drop in zinc prices below US$2,000/t would significantly inflate the reported copper AISC and pressure margins.

**Quantified Sensitivity Impacts (Rule of Thumb):**
*   **Copper Price:** Every US$500/t move in the long-term copper price impacts our SOTP fair value by approximately **R0.02/share**.
*   **USD/ZAR:** Every R1.00 move in the USD/ZAR exchange rate impacts our SOTP fair value by approximately **R0.03/share**.
*   **Zinc Price:** Every US$100/t move in the long-term zinc price impacts our SOTP fair value by approximately **R0.01/share**.

**Expansion & Delivery Catalysts (What will trigger an upgrade):**
1.  **First Drawdown of Tranche A:** Confirmation that the US$40m has been received (expected end-Oct 2026).
2.  **Award of Key Contracts:** Final execution of the B-O-O-T concentrator contract with ENPROTEC and the mining contract.
3.  **Deeps Funding Solution:** A credible funding package for the US$210m Deeps phase, potentially involving a partner or a larger equity raise.
4.  **Okiep Optimisation Study:** Release of an updated, post-optimisation feasibility study for Okiep with a revised NPV and capital estimate.
5.  **Resource Upgrade:** Conversion of a material portion of Inferred resources at Prieska or Okiep into the Reserve category.

**Upcoming Milestones & Reporting Dates:**
*   **End-October 2026:** Expected first drawdown of Glencore Tranche A.
*   **Q4 CY2026:** Commencement of site mobilisation and early works at Prieska.
*   **February 2027:** Release of H1 FY27 interim results.
