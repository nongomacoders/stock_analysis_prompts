### Tax Mechanics Verification (South African Section 36 & Statutory Rate vs. Gold Formula)

Per **Core Operating Rule 4** and **Note 25 (p. 119)**:

* **Statutory Corporate Tax Rate vs. Gold Formula:** While South African gold miners are taxed under the statutory Gold Mining Formula ($Y = 33 - 165/X$), where $X$ is the ratio of taxable mining profit to mining revenue (yielding 0% tax when profit margin $X \le 5\%$), Orion's South African subsidiaries (`PCZM` and `NOM`) are base metals (copper-zinc) entities subject to the **27.0% South African corporate income tax rate** alongside the **Mineral and Petroleum Resources Royalty Act (MPRRA)** royalty (minimum 0.5% to ~2.5% of gross sales during initial ramp-up).
* **100% Upfront Section 36 Capital Redemption Shield:** Under Section 36 of the South African Income Tax Act (applicable to both gold and base-metal mines), **100% of qualifying capital expenditure** is redeemable upfront against taxable mining income before any cash corporate tax is payable. As confirmed in **Note 25 (p. 119)**, Orion entered FY27E with **ZAR1,475 million (~A$128.7 million)** in unredeemed capital expenditure carried forward, plus **ZAR32.57 million (~A$2.85m)** in SA assessed losses. Adding FY27E–FY28E development capex expands the cumulative Section 36 tax shield to **>ZAR3.7 billion** (Scenario A) or **>ZAR2.3 billion** (Scenario B), resulting in **ZAR 0 cash income tax** in both FY27E and FY28E under either the 27% statutory rate or the Gold Formula ($X < 5\% \implies Y = 0\%$).

## 4. Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) DCF Valuation Table & Equity Bridge

To construct an institutional-grade Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) Net Asset Valuation, we start from the external March 2025 DFS post-tax project NPVs (**A$568m** for Prieska and **A$75m** for Okiep) and apply three rigorous forensic adjustments mandated by our Stage 1 and Stage 2 audit findings:

1. **Reserve-to-LOM & Execution Risk Haircut (P/NAV Multiple):** Because **34.2% of Prieska's LOM Cu** and **38.4% of its LOM Zn** rely on un-upgraded Indicated/Inferred resources rather than Probable Reserves, and because peak capital (**A$578m**) remains underfunded with Glencore CPs still pending, we apply a **0.55x development & reserve-conversion risk multiple** to Prieska's unrisked NPV. For Okiep—where LOM recovered Cu (**78.3kt**) exceeds 100% of *in-situ* Probable Reserves (**71.2kt**), Measured Resources were downgraded to Probable due to insufficient cost/modifying factor confidence, and FID is subordinated to Prieska—we apply a **0.40x pre-optimisation risk multiple**.


2. **Exact Attributable Ownership Adjustment:** Per **Page 6** and **Note 14 (p. 112)**, we multiply Prieska's risked project value by Orion's **53.32%** direct beneficial shareholding (plus the **63.32%** consolidated parent share adjustment via the **A$39.10m / R445.0m** vendor-financed BEE preference shares and **A$1.35m / R15.3m** BEE loan receivable, risk-weighted at 50% for future dividend recovery). For Okiep, we apply Orion's **50.63% (~51%)** effective ownership and deduct the risked PV of the **ZAR97.05m** contingent Agterskot liability.


3. **Full Balance Sheet Net Debt & Q4 FY27E Dilutive Equity Raise Bridge:** We deduct all on-balance-sheet loans (**A$30.90m / R351.6m**), lease & instalment liabilities (**A$2.57m / R29.3m**), and the Triple Flag 0.8% GRR contract liability (**A$12.81m / R145.8m**), add unrestricted cash (**A$13.74m / R156.4m**), and incorporate the mandatory **Q4 FY27E equity placement** required by **Note 2.1.3 (p. 92)** (modeled as **A$18.0m / R207.4m** raised at a 10% discount to A$0.022 = A$0.020 / R0.23 per share, issuing **900.0m new shares** to bring post-raise share count to **9,854.4m shares**).

## 6. Actionable Portfolio Monitoring Triggers & Catalysts ("What to Watch For That Will Move the Stock")

### A. Operational & Financial Red Lines (Downside Triggers)

1. **Glencore Tranche A Condition Precedent (CP) Slippage Beyond Q2 FY27 (31 December 2026):** First concentrate from Prieska Uppers is gated at **~13 months post-funding close**. If intercreditor and offtake CPs between Glencore, Triple Flag, and the IDC slip past 31 December 2026, Uppers Phase 1 revenue will miss H1 CY28 entirely, pushing FY28E EBITDA deeper into negative territory and forcing a larger, more dilutive equity placement prior to Q4 FY27.


2. **Zinc By-Product Price Compression Below US$2,200/t or TC/RC Spike (NSR < 65%):** Because Prieska's Deeps economics rely on **610,630t of LOM zinc** (**2.87x** copper tonnage) at a **69.7% NSR factor** to depress headline copper AISC to **US$0.94/lb**, a structural drop in zinc prices below US$2,200/t or higher smelter treatment charges will breach the Deeps debt-service coverage ratio required to unlock Glencore's US$160m–US$210m Tranche B facility.


3. **Underground Crown Pillar / Supergene Grade Dilution Below 1.85% Cu in Uppers Phase 1:** The Uppers Phase 1 Probable Ore Reserve is tiny (**629kt @ 2.3% Cu for 14.5kt contained Cu**) and sits adjacent to historic mined-out voids. Any stope dilution exceeding 15% (dropping mill feed below **1.85% Cu**) or refractory oxide recovery penalties below **80% Cu recovery** will push Uppers C1 cash costs above **US$2.65/lb** and eliminate Phase 1 cash generation.


4. **Hutchings Shaft Dewatering / Eskom Phased Grid Connection Delays:** Deeps Phase 2 requires dewatering flooded underground workings below the 275m level and phased Eskom power upgrades alongside Orange River water treatment expansion with the Siyathemba Municipality. Any municipal water/power bottleneck or shaft steelwork failure will stall Deeps ramp-up beyond 2030.



### B. Quantified Sensitivity Rules of Thumb

* **Copper Price Sensitivity:** Every **US$500/t (US$0.23/lb)** move in the long-term copper price impacts FY28E Uppers revenue by **R17.3m**, steady-state Prieska + Okiep annual EBITDA by **R265m**, and Orion's risked attributable SOTP equity fair value by **R0.023 per share (2.3 ZAR cents)**.
* **Zinc Price Sensitivity:** Every **US$250/t (US$0.11/lb)** move in the zinc price shifts Prieska's Deeps by-product credit by **US$0.22/lb Cu**, alters steady-state annual EBITDA by **R192m**, and moves Orion's risked SOTP fair value by **R0.016 per share (1.6 ZAR cents)**.
* **USD/ZAR Exchange Rate Sensitivity:** Every **R1.00/US$** depreciation in the Rand (holding USD metal prices constant) improves FY28E Uppers operating cash flow by **R10.6m**, expands steady-state annual EBITDA by **R310m**, and increases Orion's JSE fair value by **R0.024 per share (2.4 ZAR cents)**.
* **Equity Dilution Sensitivity:** Every **A$10.0m (R115m)** raised via parent equity at **A$0.018–A$0.020/share** issues **500m–555m new ordinary shares (+5.6% to +6.2% dilution)**, reducing upside SOTP per-share recovery by **~1.1 ZAR cents per share**.

### C. Expansion & Delivery Catalysts Required for an Institutional Upgrade

1. **Unconditional Financial Close & First Drawdown of Glencore Tranche A (US$40m) + Triple Flag Intercreditor Sign-Off:** Satisfying all outstanding CPs and drawing the initial US$40m Uppers facility without further parent-level equity dilution would remove the immediate execution discount, lifting our P/NAV multiple on Prieska from **0.55x to 0.70x** (adding **+R0.05/share** to fair value).


2. **Infill Drilling Conversion of Prieska's 10Mt Deep Sulphide Inferred Resource into Probable Reserves:** Converting the **72.8kt Cu and 234.6kt Zn** non-reserve LOM deficit into JORC Probable Ore Reserves via the 22,000m underground resource definition program (`D008.3`, p. 51) would validate the 13.2-year LOM schedule and add **+R0.04/share**.


3. **Publication of the Updated Okiep Flat Mines Optimisation DFS:** Demonstrating lower waste-incline development capex and incorporating high-grade Flat Mine East intersections (**49.35m @ 5.05% Cu** in OFMED153; **7.88m @ 9.24% Cu** in OFMED157) into an expanded Probable Ore Reserve that fully covers LOM recovered metal would trigger a re-rating of Okiep from **0.40x to 0.75x NAV** (**+R0.015/share**).
