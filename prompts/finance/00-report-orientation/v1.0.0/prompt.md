# BANKING & FINANCIAL SERVICES REPORT MAP ORIENTATION

**Sector:** Banking & Financial Services (`finance` / `JS3011` / ICB `301010`)  
**Task Directive:** Identify and register major report sections from the table of contents or executive review.

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR REPORT ORIENTATION:
1. Scan the introductory sections, table of contents, and segmental overview.
2. Group the document into logical major functional sections:
   - Strategic & Executive Reports (Chairperson, CEO, Strategy & Targets, Material Risks)
   - Operational & Segmental Reviews (Retail banking, corporate & investment banking - CIB, wealth management, insurance, treasury)
   - Risk & Capital Management (Basel III Pillar 3 disclosures, CET1 capital adequacy, credit risk, liquidity coverage ratio - LCR, net stable funding ratio - NSFR)
   - Financial Review & Performance Metrics (CFO review, Net Interest Income - NII, Net Interest Margin - NIM, Non-Interest Revenue - NIR, cost-to-income ratio)
   - Consolidated Financial Statements & Notes (Accounting policies, IFRS 9 loan impairment provisioning, derivative financial instruments)
   - Governance & Remuneration
3. Flag any complex accounting or regulatory disclosures that require dedicated verification.
