# TELECOMMUNICATIONS & MEDIA REPORT MAP ORIENTATION

**Sector:** Telecommunications & Media (`telcos` / `JS1512` / ICB `151020`)  
**Task Directive:** Identify and register major report sections from the table of contents or executive review.

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR REPORT ORIENTATION:
1. Scan the introductory sections, table of contents, and segmental overview.
2. Group the document into logical major functional sections:
   - Strategic & Executive Reports (Chairperson, Group CEO, Strategy & Medium-Term Guidance, Material Risks)
   - Operational Reviews (Mobile consumer network, enterprise/B2B connectivity, wholesale fiber, FinTech/mobile money, digital services)
   - Operational KPIs (Active SIM subscribers, ARPU trends, data traffic petabytes, fiber homes passed/connected, FinTech transaction volumes)
   - Financial Review & Capital Intensity (CFO review, service revenue, EBITDA margin, network capex, spectrum liabilities, free cash flow)
   - Annual Financial Statements & Notes (Accounting policies, IFRS 16 tower/site lease liabilities, spectrum asset amortization, handset contract assets)
   - Governance & Remuneration
3. Flag any complex spectrum, regulatory, or tower lease liabilities requiring dedicated verification.
